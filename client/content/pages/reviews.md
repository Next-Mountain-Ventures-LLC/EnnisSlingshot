## We're building this page honestly

We ran our first full season in 2026 and are still early in collecting reviews — so rather than invent quotes or backfill this page with anything that isn't real, we're leaving it as what it is: a work in progress. As riders leave reviews and share their stories, they'll appear here, unedited and attributed.

## Known so far

We've hosted rides for a range of occasions already, including at least one birthday and one anniversary — the kind of celebrations that show up again and again across Slingshot rentals nationally. We're not going to put words in anyone's mouth before they've said them; check back as the 2027 season builds.

## How to leave a review

If you've ridden with us, we'd genuinely like to hear how it went — good or bad. After your experience, you'll get a follow-up email with a link to leave a review. You can also leave one directly on our Google Business Profile, which is the fastest way to help other riders find us and decide if this is right for them.

## What will appear here

As real reviews and rider stories come in, we'll feature them on this page with the rider's name (or initials, if preferred) and the occasion, the same way the category's best-known reviews read elsewhere — a birthday, an anniversary, a girls' trip, a first-timer nervous about the drive who ended up loving it. Nothing here will be written for effect; if a season is quiet, this page stays quiet too.
