## Rode with us? Tell us how it went

We'd love to hear about your ride — good or bad. Email [info@ennisslingshot.com](mailto:info@ennisslingshot.com) with how it went, the occasion, and a photo if you took one, and let us know if we can share it here with your first name or initials. After your experience, you'll also get a follow-up email from us with a link to leave a review.

Your feedback helps us keep improving the ride, and it helps other drivers decide whether a Slingshot experience on the Bluebonnet Trails is right for them. Haven't ridden yet? The [FAQ](/faq/) covers what to expect.
