## Where we meet you

Every Ennis Slingshot Experience ride starts at the Ennis Welcome Center:

**Ennis Welcome Center**\
201 NW Main St\
Ennis, TX 75119

[Get directions in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&destination=Ennis+Welcome+Center+201+NW+Main+St+Ennis+TX+75119)

This is the same building where the official Ennis Bluebonnet Trail maps are handed out during trail season. Arrive 15 minutes before your booked time for check-in, orientation, and a safety briefing before you drive. See [About](/about/) for why we chose this as our starting point, and [Requirements](/slingshot-rental/requirements/) for what to bring.

## Get in touch

**Email:** [info@ennisslingshot.com](mailto:info@ennisslingshot.com)

Questions before you book? Email us — groups, gift cards, or anything you want to know before you pick a date — and we'll get right back to you. Already booked? Reply to your confirmation email; it's the fastest way to reach us about a specific booking.

## Hours during trail season

The Ennis Welcome Center — our meeting point — typically keeps these hours during bloom season: Monday–Friday 8 am–6 pm, Saturday 9 am–5 pm, Sunday 11 am–4 pm. Our own booking windows run within those hours; see [Book](/book/) for available time slots on your date.

## Directions

Ennis is about 35 miles south of downtown Dallas on I-45, roughly a 37-minute drive. See our [day-trip guide](/ennis/day-trip-from-dallas/) for the full route and drive times from other DFW cities.
