## Seasonal coverage of the Ennis Bluebonnet Trails

This category covers everything tied to bluebonnet season in Ennis — bloom updates as the trails open each April, photo-spot guides, festival news, and the occasional look at how Ennis compares to other Texas bluebonnet destinations. Posts here are seasonal and dated on purpose; for the evergreen, always-current picture of the trails, loops, and festival, see our [Ennis Bluebonnet Trails & Festival guide](/bluebonnets/).

If you're planning a visit around peak bloom, this is the place to check for the latest updates before you go. And if you want to see the trails from behind the wheel of a Polaris Slingshot instead of through a windshield, the [Bluebonnet Trail Experience](/slingshot-rental/bluebonnet-trail-experience/) runs the full April season.
