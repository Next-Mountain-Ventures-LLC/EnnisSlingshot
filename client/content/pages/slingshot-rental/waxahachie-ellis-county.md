## Our home county, and a natural Scarborough Faire pairing

Ennis Slingshot Experience is based in Ellis County — the same county as Waxahachie, about 15 miles away. If you're already planning a Waxahachie trip, especially during Scarborough Renaissance Festival season, adding an Ennis Slingshot ride is a short drive, not a separate outing. This page is part of our [Slingshot rental lineup](/slingshot-rental/).

## Pair your ride with Scarborough Renaissance Festival

Scarborough Renaissance Festival runs weekends from April through late May in Waxahachie — a 35-acre village with roughly 200 shoppes, jousting, and live entertainment that draws well over 100,000 Texas visitors across the season. It overlaps almost entirely with our April Slingshot season, and the two make a natural full-day pairing: ride the [Bluebonnet Trail Experience](/slingshot-rental/bluebonnet-trail-experience/) in the morning while the light and temperatures are best, then head to the Faire for the afternoon — or the reverse, if you'd rather end your day behind the wheel.

## Make it a day

- **Morning trail ride.** Book the [Bluebonnet Trail Experience](/slingshot-rental/bluebonnet-trail-experience/) or, if you're short on time, the 1-hour [Drive & Go](/slingshot-rental/drive-and-go/).
- **Afternoon at Scarborough Faire.** About 20 minutes from the Welcome Center, running weekends April through late May.
- **Explore Ennis itself.** Historic downtown, local restaurants, and more are covered on [Things to Do in Ennis, TX](/ennis/).
- **See the bluebonnets.** Ellis County — Ennis specifically — is the Texas Legislature's designated Bluebonnet Capital of Texas. The full trail map and bloom timing are on the [Ennis Bluebonnet Trails guide](/bluebonnets/).

## Planning your visit

If you're timing a trip around Scarborough Renaissance Festival, book your Slingshot slot first — festival crowds and parking build through the day, so an early ride followed by an afternoon at the Faire tends to work better than the reverse. Either way, give yourself the full day: between the trails, the Faire, and downtown Waxahachie, there's more here than a rushed afternoon can cover.

## When to come

The Ennis Bluebonnet Trails run April 1–30, with peak bloom typically around the third week of the month — timing that lines up well with the opening weekends of Scarborough Renaissance Festival. Book early if you're planning around a specific festival weekend, since April slots fill up.

## Ellis County beyond the Faire

Scarborough Renaissance Festival is the biggest draw pulling visitors through Ellis County every spring, but it's not the only reason to be here. Waxahachie's historic courthouse square and Victorian-era downtown are worth a walk-through on their own, and the county overall has a deep well of small-town Texas charm that pairs naturally with an afternoon on the Bluebonnet Trails. If you're building a longer weekend around either destination, treat Ennis and Waxahachie as two stops on the same trip rather than picking one.

## What you'll actually be driving

Every ride is in a Polaris Slingshot with AutoDrive — an automatic transmission, no clutch, so it handles like a car — powered by Polaris's ProStar 2.0L four-cylinder engine, up to 204 hp depending on trim. Two people ride side by side in an open cockpit. First-time drivers get a short orientation and a supervised practice loop before heading out onto the trail route on their own.
