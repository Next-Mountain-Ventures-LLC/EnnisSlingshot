## Every price, no deposit, no hidden fees

Slingshot rental prices from Ennis Slingshot Experience run **$69.99 to $169**, and every package includes insurance and fuel with no security deposit. That's the whole answer — what follows is the breakdown by package and how it stacks up against a typical Texas Slingshot rental. For the full picture of what each package includes, start with the [Slingshot rental hub](/slingshot-rental/).

## What's included in every price

- **Comprehensive insurance**, with a maximum of $500 out-of-pocket if something goes wrong.
- **Fuel** for the full duration of your rental.
- **A safety orientation and supervised practice loop** before you're on your own on the trail.
- **A trail map** for the Bluebonnet Trail Experience, Date Night, and Group Convoy packages.

Full driver requirements, age minimums, and cancellation policy are on [Requirements](/slingshot-rental/requirements/).

## How we compare to a typical DFW Slingshot rental

Slingshot rentals based in the Dallas–Fort Worth metro typically charge around $150 for one hour and $200 for two hours, plus a refundable deposit of $150–$500 depending on your age and the length of the rental. Our shortest package, Drive & Go, costs less than half of a comparable hourly rate — and unlike a typical DFW rental, there's no deposit theater at checkout.

| | Typical DFW Slingshot rental | Ennis Slingshot Experience |
|---|---|---|
| 1-hour rate | ~$150 | $69.99 (Drive & Go) |
| 2-hour rate | ~$200 | $79 solo / $149 two-up |
| Deposit | $150–$500 | None |
| Insurance | Often extra or bundled at a fee | Included in every package |

We don't say this to claim we're the cheapest option anywhere in Texas — some scenic-tour operators elsewhere in the state price similarly. We say it because the friction that keeps people from trying a Slingshot — deposits, add-on insurance fees, pickup logistics — is exactly what our pricing removes.

## Why we price it this way

We didn't set out to be the cheapest Slingshot rental in Texas — a couple of scenic-tour operators elsewhere in the state land in a similar range. We set out to remove the specific friction that keeps people from ever trying one: a deposit that ties up your card for days, a mandatory insurance upcharge on top of the listed rate, and pickup logistics that make a two-hour idea feel like a half-day project. Every price on this page is the actual total, aside from the two optional add-ons — a helmet or a photo package — that you choose yourself.

## A note on Festival Weekend pricing

During the Ennis Bluebonnet Trails Festival — historically the second or third weekend of April — demand for Slingshot rides in Ennis is higher than any other weekend of the season, and we expect availability to be genuinely tight.
