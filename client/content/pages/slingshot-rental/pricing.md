## How we compare to a typical DFW Slingshot rental

Slingshot rentals based in the Dallas–Fort Worth metro typically charge around $150 for one hour and $200 for two hours, plus a refundable deposit of $150–$500 depending on your age and the length of the rental. Our shortest package, Drive & Go, costs less than half of a comparable hourly rate — and unlike a typical DFW rental, there's no deposit theater at checkout.

## Why we price it this way

We didn't set out to be the cheapest Slingshot rental in Texas — a couple of scenic-tour operators elsewhere in the state land in a similar range. We set out to remove the specific friction that keeps people from ever trying one: a deposit that ties up your card for days, a mandatory insurance upcharge on top of the listed rate, and pickup logistics that make a two-hour idea feel like a half-day project. Every price on this page is the actual total, aside from an optional Bluetooth helmet that you choose yourself.
