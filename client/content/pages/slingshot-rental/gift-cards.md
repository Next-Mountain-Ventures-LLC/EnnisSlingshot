## Give the experience, not the guesswork

A Slingshot experience gift card lets someone else pick their own April date — no guessing at their schedule, no risk of a birthday or holiday gift sitting unused in a drawer. It's part of our [Slingshot rental lineup](/slingshot-rental/), and it's built for exactly the kind of gifting people love most: a surprise for a partner's birthday, an anniversary, or a "just because" gesture for someone who'd never book this for themselves.

Most people would rather receive an experience than another thing to find a shelf for — and a Slingshot ride is an easy way to give someone in Dallas something to look forward to. A gift card bridges the gap between holiday shopping and our April season: buy it now, redeem it once the bluebonnets are blooming.

## Available gift card options

| Package | Value |
|---|---|
| Drive & Go (1 hour, 1 driver) | $69.99 |
| Bluebonnet Trail Experience — Solo (2 hours, 1 driver) | $79 |
| Bluebonnet Trail Experience — Driver + Rider (2 hours, driver + 1 passenger) | $149 |

Not sure which package they'd pick? Ask us about a gift card for a set dollar amount instead. You can also add Bluetooth helmets ($25 each) to a gift card if you want to build in an extra.

## How to order

Email [info@ennisslingshot.com](mailto:info@ennisslingshot.com?subject=Slingshot%20gift%20card) and tell us which package you'd like to give — Drive & Go, Solo, or Driver + Rider — and we'll get your gift card set up. It's priced the same as booking directly; there's no markup for gifting it.

## When to buy

- **November–December.** Holiday gifting season — a gift card under the tree gives them an April adventure to look forward to.
- **Late January–February.** A Slingshot ride makes a standout Valentine's Day gift for someone who has everything.
- **Birthdays and anniversaries.** Whenever the occasion lands, a gift card gives them April's bluebonnet season to look forward to.

## Redeeming a gift card

When they're ready, the recipient emails [info@ennisslingshot.com](mailto:info@ennisslingshot.com) with their gift card to pick their own April date and time. Whoever drives still needs to be 21 or older and go through the same quick driver-verification process as any other booking — that part isn't something a gift card can skip, since it's tied to insurance approval, not payment.

## Why a gift card beats guessing a date

Experience gifts only work if the recipient can actually use them, and nothing kills a well-meant gift faster than picking a date that doesn't fit someone's schedule. Because our season is a single month, that risk is higher than it would be for a year-round business — which is exactly why the gift card leaves the date up to them. Buy it in December for a February anniversary or a spring birthday, and let the recipient lock in their own April slot when they can check their calendar for real.

## Who gift cards work best for

- **The "surprise planner."** Often someone buying for a partner who would never book this for themselves — a gift card removes the pressure of guessing the right day.
- **Long-distance family and friends.** If you're not local to Ennis, a gift card is easier to give than trying to coordinate an exact date from out of town.
- **Corporate or team gifts.** Ask us about gift cards for a team — a distinctive thank-you or incentive gift that lets each person choose their own date.

## What to pair it with

If you're buying for a couple, the two-up [Bluebonnet Trail Experience](/slingshot-rental/bluebonnet-trail-experience/) ($149 for two) makes a great [date night](/slingshot-rental/date-night/) gift. If you're not sure the recipient knows what a Slingshot ride even involves, point them to the [Bluebonnet Trail Experience](/slingshot-rental/bluebonnet-trail-experience/) page, or the full [Ennis Bluebonnet Trails guide](/bluebonnets/) so they can see what the drive actually looks like.

## A note on timing

Because we only operate in April, a gift card bought in the fall or winter is really a promise of a spring afternoon — which is part of what makes it work as a gift in the first place. It gives the recipient something to look forward to across the winter, not just a coupon to redeem this week. If you're buying close to the season itself, that's fine too; just check availability on [our booking page](/book/), since April dates fill up fast.
