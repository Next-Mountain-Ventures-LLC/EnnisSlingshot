## Our flagship ride: two hours on the Ennis Bluebonnet Trails

The Bluebonnet Trail Experience is a 2-hour, self-drive Slingshot ride through the Ennis Bluebonnet Trails — **$79 solo, $149 for a driver and one passenger**. It's the flagship package in our [Slingshot rental lineup](/slingshot-rental/): enough time to cover a real stretch of the trails, stop for photos, and actually feel like a drive rather than a quick lap.

Ennis is the Texas Legislature's designated Bluebonnet Capital of Texas, with more than 40 miles of mapped driving loops maintained by the Ennis Garden Club. We've built a curated 2-hour route through those trails, tuned for open-cockpit driving and scenic stops, and every rider gets a printed trail map for the glove box.

Only the driver goes through driver approval — a verification link emailed after booking, usually cleared within 24 hours. A passenger just shows up. No motorcycle license is required for the driver: Texas classifies the Slingshot as an autocycle, and Tex. Transp. Code §521.085(b) allows a standard Class C license to operate it. Drivers must be 21 or older. Full detail on age, approval, and what to bring is on [Requirements](/slingshot-rental/requirements/).

## The route

Your route runs through the official Bluebonnet Trail loops, with stops planned at some of the best-known wildflower spots in Ennis. Bloom timing varies with the weather, but the trails are officially open April 1–30, and peak bloom usually lands around the third week of April. Booking mid-month gives you the best odds of a full field of blue. For the complete picture — the three official loops, historic background, and where to look for flowers — see the [Ennis Bluebonnet Trails & Festival guide](/bluebonnets/).

Choose solo if you want the vehicle to yourself, or if you're the try-before-you-buy type who wants to focus entirely on the drive. ## How it drives

Every vehicle is a Polaris Slingshot with AutoDrive — an automatic transmission, no clutch, so it drives like a car — powered by Polaris's ProStar 2.0L four-cylinder engine, up to 204 hp depending on trim. Before you're on your own, we run through the controls and a supervised practice loop so you're comfortable with the steering, throttle, and braking feel before you're out on the open trail roads.

## What a 2-hour window actually covers

Two hours breaks down roughly like this: 15 minutes of check-in and orientation before your official start time, a supervised practice loop to get comfortable with the controls, then close to 90 minutes of actual trail driving with time built in for at least one photo stop. It's enough time to cover a meaningful stretch of the Bluebonnet Trail loops without feeling rushed — longer than Drive & Go's single hour, and long enough that most riders don't want to be back in the car the second the flowers come into view.

## What's not included

This is a self-drive experience with a curated route, not a guided tour with someone riding along, and it doesn't include the golden-hour photo stop built into [Date Night](/slingshot-rental/date-night/) or the multi-vehicle convoy setup of [Groups](/slingshot-rental/groups/).
