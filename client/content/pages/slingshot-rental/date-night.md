## A ride through the bluebonnets, built for two

A Slingshot date night is our 2-hour [Bluebonnet Trail Experience](/slingshot-rental/bluebonnet-trail-experience/) for two — **$149 for a driver and passenger**, with time to pull over for photos together in the bluebonnets. It's the most occasion-focused way to ride in our [Slingshot rental lineup](/slingshot-rental/) — built for anniversaries, birthdays, and the kind of date that's meant to be remembered, not just filled.

An open cockpit, a warm April day, and fields of wildflowers on both sides of the road — it's the kind of date you'll actually talk about afterward. Want the softest light? Book a late-afternoon slot for golden-hour color over the fields.

Only the driver needs to go through driver approval — a verification link emailed right after booking, usually cleared within 24 hours; a full refund if you're not approved. Drivers must be 21 or older with a valid driver's license (Texas or out-of-state) — no motorcycle license required, since Texas classifies the Slingshot as an autocycle under Tex. Transp. Code §521.085(b). Full policy detail is on [Requirements](/slingshot-rental/requirements/).

## Why couples love it

A Slingshot ride is one of the most popular ways to celebrate a birthday or anniversary — something more memorable than dinner and a movie. Driving it through the Ennis Bluebonnet Trails in April, with wildflowers on both sides of the road, turns it into a date you'll both remember.

## Make it a full evening

Pair your ride with dinner in historic downtown Ennis before or after your slot — the courthouse square area has local restaurants within a few minutes of the Welcome Center. If you're coming from Dallas or Fort Worth and want to build a full evening around it, [Near Dallas](/slingshot-rental/near-dallas/) has drive-time and itinerary suggestions, and the [Ennis Bluebonnet Trails guide](/bluebonnets/) covers where the best photo fields are if you want to scout ahead.

## Photos worth keeping

Your trail route includes recommended stops at scenic bluebonnet fields, so you can pull over, step out, and get a handful of real photos together without feeling rushed. Bring a small phone tripod if you want both of you in the shot — it's the difference between "we drove past some flowers" and having an actual photo from the day.

## Book early

April weekends fill up fast, especially around peak bloom and festival weekend — the 2027 Ennis Bluebonnet Trails Festival is expected April 17–19, 2027 (dates subject to change). If you have a specific date in mind — an anniversary, a birthday, a proposal — book a few weeks ahead.
