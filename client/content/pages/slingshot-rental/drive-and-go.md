## The fastest way to find out what a Slingshot feels like

Drive & Go is a 60-minute, self-drive Slingshot experience from Ennis, TX for **$69.99** — the shortest, most affordable way to get behind the wheel. It's part of our [full Slingshot rental lineup](/slingshot-rental/), built for people who want real seat time without committing to a full afternoon: a curious first-timer, someone cross-shopping a Slingshot purchase, or a couple squeezing a quick adventure into a busy day trip.

Unlike a dealership test drive, you're not circling a parking lot with a salesperson in the passenger seat. You get a short orientation, a supervised practice loop, and then an hour on real roads through the Ennis Bluebonnet Trails — on your own.

- **The try-before-you-buy driver.** If you're weighing a Slingshot purchase, an hour of real driving tells you more than any brochure. It's a no-dealer test drive on open roads, not a showroom loop.
- **The curious first-timer with limited time.** Maybe you're in Ennis for the bluebonnets and want a taste of the drive without the full 2-hour commitment.

If you want more time on the trails and the option to bring a passenger, step up to the [2-hour Bluebonnet Trail Experience](/slingshot-rental/bluebonnet-trail-experience/), which covers more of the route at a comparable per-hour price.

## How it drives

Every Slingshot in our fleet is a Polaris Slingshot with AutoDrive — an automatic transmission with no clutch, so it drives like a car: steering wheel, gas pedal, brake pedal. It's powered by Polaris's ProStar 2.0L four-cylinder engine, with up to 204 hp depending on trim. Before you head out, we walk through the controls and run a supervised practice loop so the transition from "car" to "Slingshot" happens with someone watching, not on your own on the trail.

## The route

Your Drive & Go route is a shortened version of our Bluebonnet Trail route — enough open road and scenery to get a real feel for the vehicle, with one recommended stop for photos. If you're visiting during bloom season (the trails run April 1–30, typically peaking around the third week), you'll see the wildflowers Ennis is known for along the way. For the full trail picture — loops, timing, and where to look — see the [Ennis Bluebonnet Trails guide](/bluebonnets/).

## What to expect when you arrive

Plan to be at the Ennis Welcome Center 15 minutes before your slot. We'll check your ID against your driver approval, run through the controls, and take you through a short supervised practice loop before turning you loose. Because AutoDrive behaves a little differently than a normal car transmission — it can downshift on its own to slow the vehicle, which catches first-timers off guard more than anything else — that practice loop matters even for experienced drivers. From there, your hour on the trail is entirely your own.

## What Drive & Go doesn't include

To keep this honest: 60 minutes doesn't cover the entire Bluebonnet Trail system, and it's a one-driver package, so it's not the pick if you want to bring someone along in the passenger seat. It also doesn't include the guided photo stop that comes with [Golden Hour Date Night](/slingshot-rental/date-night/) or the trail map that ships with the 2-hour packages. If any of that matters more to you than speed and price, the [Bluebonnet Trail Experience](/slingshot-rental/bluebonnet-trail-experience/) is the better starting point.

## Add a helmet

A Bluetooth communication helmet rents for $25. It's not legally required for most adult riders — Texas treats an autocycle like a motorcycle for helmet purposes, with an exemption for riders 21 and older who've completed a safety course or carry qualifying health insurance — but we strongly recommend one regardless, since it's an open cockpit at speed. Full helmet and age rules are on [Requirements](/slingshot-rental/requirements/).
