## What you need: a driver's license, no motorcycle license required

To drive with us, you need a valid Texas (or out-of-state) driver's license and to be 21 or older — that's it. No motorcycle license, endorsement, or class is required. Texas law classifies the Polaris Slingshot as an autocycle, and Tex. Transp. Code §521.085(b) specifically allows a standard Class C driver's license to operate one. This is one of the most misunderstood facts in the entire category, and it's worth stating plainly: if you can legally drive a car in Texas, you can legally drive a Slingshot with us.

This page is the full policy reference for our [Slingshot rental lineup](/slingshot-rental/) — age, insurance, approval, helmets, and weather, all in one place.

## Age requirements

- **Drivers:** 21 or older, with a valid driver's license.
- **Passengers:** No strict minimum age, but every rider needs to sit securely and wear a seatbelt properly for the full ride.

## Insurance & driver approval

Comprehensive insurance is included in every package, with a maximum of $500 out-of-pocket if something goes wrong with the vehicle. After you book, we email a verification link to confirm your license and complete a quick insurance-approval check — it usually clears within 24 hours. If you're not approved for any reason, you receive a full refund; there's no cancellation fee for a failed approval.

We don't require a security deposit on top of this. Your package price is the total cost of the rental (aside from optional add-ons).

## Helmets

Texas helmet law for autocycles follows motorcycle rules under Tex. Transp. Code §661.0015(b): riders under 21 must wear a helmet, and riders 21 or older are exempt if they've completed a motorcycle operator safety course or carry a health insurance plan that covers injuries from a collision (§661.003). A stock Slingshot has an open cockpit, so that exemption applies the same way it would on a motorcycle.

Even where it's not legally required, we strongly recommend wearing a helmet — it's an open vehicle on public roads, and the wind, sun, and speed are part of the experience. We rent Bluetooth communication helmets for $25 each so a driver and passenger can talk to each other along the route.

## What to wear

- **Closed-toe shoes**, since you'll be stepping in and out of an open cockpit and operating pedals.
- **Sunglasses or eye protection** — there's no windshield to speak of, and Texas sun in April is bright even at 78°F average highs.
- **Layers**, especially for later slots — evenings cool off as the sun drops.
- **Sunscreen**, particularly for the 2-hour and longer packages.

## Rescheduling & cancellation

- **Rescheduling** is free up to 7 days before your booked date, subject to availability.
- **Weather or mechanical cancellations** don't come with a refund, but you'll get a free reschedule to another date. We run rain or shine unless conditions are genuinely unsafe to drive.
- **Failed driver approval** results in a full refund automatically.

## What to bring

- **Your driver's license** — the physical card, not just a photo of it.
- **Closed-toe shoes**, sunglasses, and sunscreen (see "what to wear" above).
- **A phone** if you want your own photos along the route, in addition to the guided stop that comes with some packages.

Every vehicle is a Polaris Slingshot with AutoDrive — an automatic transmission with no clutch, so it drives like a car — powered by Polaris's ProStar 2.0L four-cylinder engine, up to 204 hp depending on trim. Before you're on your own, every driver gets a short orientation and a supervised practice loop.

## If you're booking for someone else

A gift-card purchaser doesn't need to meet any of these requirements themselves — only the person who actually drives does. If you're buying a [gift card](/slingshot-rental/gift-cards/) for a partner, friend, or family member, make sure they're 21 or older with a valid license before you commit to a specific package, since that's what determines eligibility, not who paid for it.
