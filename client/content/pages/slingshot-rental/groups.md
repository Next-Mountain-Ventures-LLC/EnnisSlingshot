## Two Slingshots, one time slot, up to 4 people

Our group ride is built for birthdays, bachelorette outings, and small groups of friends who want to drive the Ennis Bluebonnet Trails together. Our fleet is two Polaris Slingshots, so booking both for the same time slot means up to 4 people (two drivers, two passengers) riding side by side in the same 2-hour window — at our standard prices, **$149 per Slingshot** with a driver and passenger ($79 for a solo driver).

It's part of our [Slingshot rental lineup](/slingshot-rental/), built for the occasions people love to celebrate together — a small circle of friends, a birthday crew, a bachelorette weekend.

## What's included

| | Detail |
|---|---|
| Price | $149 per Slingshot with a passenger, $79 solo (up to 2 Slingshots / 4 people per slot) |
| Duration | 2 hours (arrive 15 minutes early) |
| Fleet | 2 Polaris Slingshots, each seating driver + 1 passenger |
| Insurance | Comprehensive, included per vehicle, $500 max out-of-pocket |
| Deposit | None |

Each vehicle needs its own driver, 21 or older with a valid Texas driver's license — no motorcycle license required, since the Slingshot is classified as an autocycle under Tex. Transp. Code §521.085(b). Only drivers go through approval; passengers just show up. Full detail on age, approval, and policies is on [Requirements](/slingshot-rental/requirements/).

## How a group ride actually works

Both Slingshots leave the Ennis Welcome Center together and drive the Bluebonnet Trail route side by side — not a guided tour with a lead vehicle, but two cars on the same route at the same time, so your group can stop together for photos and stay in sight of each other along the way. Each driver gets the same orientation and supervised practice loop as a solo or two-up booking before the group heads out.

If your group is larger than 4 people, email [info@ennisslingshot.com](mailto:info@ennisslingshot.com) — we can often set up a second 2-hour slot the same day so the rest of your group rides too.

## Good for

- **Birthday celebrations.** A group ride through the trails, with recommended photo stops along the route, works well as the centerpiece of a birthday outing.
- **Bachelorette-style outings.** Small groups of friends looking for something more memorable than the usual bar crawl or spa day.
- **Friend groups and couples doubling up.** Two couples, or four friends splitting into pairs, riding the trails together.

If it's really just the two of you, a two-up [Bluebonnet Trail Experience](/slingshot-rental/bluebonnet-trail-experience/) — or our [Date Night](/slingshot-rental/date-night/) ideas — might be a better fit. Add a Bluetooth helmet ($25 each) so both drivers can talk to their passengers along the route.

## Why we cap it at two vehicles

We'd rather be straight about our fleet than oversell it: two Slingshots means two Slingshots, not a five-vehicle caravan. What that buys you is a manageable, close-together group experience — both cars leave together, drive together, and stop together, so nobody's left waiting for a straggler or losing sight of the group on unfamiliar rural roads. If your group is bigger than 4, we'll work with you to book a second same-day slot rather than overselling a single one.
