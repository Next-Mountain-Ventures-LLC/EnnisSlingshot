    answer: "No. Texas classifies the Polaris Slingshot as an autocycle under Tex. Transp. Code §501.008, and §521.085(b) allows a standard Class C driver's license — the same one you use for a car — to operate one. No motorcycle endorsement, class, or test is required."
  - question: "Is a helmet required to ride a Slingshot in Texas?"
    answer: "Motorcycle helmet law applies to autocycles under Tex. Transp. Code §661.0015(b) since a stock Slingshot has no enclosed cab. Under §661.003, riders under 21 must wear a helmet; riders 21 and older are exempt if they've completed a motorcycle safety course or carry health insurance covering collision injuries. We provide helmets for every rider regardless of the exemption, and recommend wearing one."
