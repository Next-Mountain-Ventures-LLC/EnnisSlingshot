## The short version

We collect the information needed to book and run your Slingshot experience, use standard analytics and advertising tools to understand and improve our site, and don't sell your personal information to third parties.

## Information we collect

- **Booking information** you provide when scheduling a rental — name, contact details, and driver-verification details required for approval — is processed through our booking platform (Acuity Scheduling) and used to run your reservation.
- **Contact information** you send us through the [contact page](/contact/) or email, used to respond to your question.
- **Site analytics**, collected automatically when you visit ennisslingshot.com — pages viewed, general location, device type, and referral source — via Google Analytics 4 (GA4).
- **Advertising and conversion data**, collected via the Meta Pixel, used to measure and improve our Facebook and Instagram advertising and to build remarketing audiences for people who've visited our site.

## How we use it

We use booking and contact information to run your reservation, communicate with you about it, and respond to questions. We use analytics and advertising data to understand how people find and use the site, and to run more relevant advertising. We don't sell your personal information to third parties.

## Cookies & tracking tools

This site uses cookies and similar technologies from Google Analytics and Meta (Facebook) Pixel to measure site usage and advertising performance. You can control cookies through your browser settings; blocking them won't affect your ability to book a rental, though it may limit some site features.

## Third-party services

Booking is handled through Acuity Scheduling, a third-party scheduling platform; their own privacy policy governs how they handle the information you submit there. Analytics and advertising data pass through Google and Meta under their respective privacy policies.

## Questions about your data

If you have questions about the information we collect or want it removed, [contact us](/contact/) and we'll respond as quickly as we can.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our tools or practices change. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent update.
