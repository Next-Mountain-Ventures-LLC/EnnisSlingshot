## The short version

We collect the information needed to book and run your Slingshot experience and to send the email and text updates you sign up for, use standard analytics and advertising tools to understand and improve our site, and don't sell your personal information to third parties.

## Information we collect

- **Booking information** you provide when scheduling a rental — name, contact details, and driver-verification details required for approval — is processed through our booking platform (Acuity Scheduling) and used to run your reservation.
- **Sign-up information** you give us through our email and text sign-up form — your first and last name, email address and, if you choose to add it, your US mobile number. See [Email and text message updates](#email-and-text-message-updates) below.
- **Contact information** you send us by email, used to respond to your question.
- **Site analytics**, collected automatically when you visit ennisslingshot.com — pages viewed, general location, device type, and referral source — via Google Analytics 4 (GA4).
- **Advertising and conversion data**, collected via the Meta Pixel, used to measure and improve our Facebook and Instagram advertising and to build remarketing audiences for people who've visited our site.

## How we use it

We use booking and contact information to run your reservation, communicate with you about it, and respond to questions. We use sign-up information to send the updates you asked for. We use analytics and advertising data to understand how people find and use the site, and to run more relevant advertising. We don't sell your personal information to third parties.

## Email and text message updates

Our sign-up form has two steps. The first collects your first name, last name and email address; the second, which is optional, collects a US mobile number for text updates and your $10-off code.

- **What we send:** Ennis Bluebonnet Festival dates, bloom reports, trail news, and offers.
- **Where it's stored:** your details are stored with our email and SMS provider, Sender, which sends these messages on our behalf.
- **We don't sell it.** We don't sell your sign-up information to anyone.
- **Your mobile number stays with us.** Mobile numbers and SMS opt-in consent are never shared with or sold to third parties or affiliates for their marketing purposes.
- **Unsubscribing:** use the unsubscribe link in any email, or reply **STOP** to any text. You can also email [info@ennisslingshot.com](mailto:info@ennisslingshot.com) and we'll remove you.

Text messages are covered by our [SMS terms](/terms/#sms-terms).

## Cookies & tracking tools

This site uses cookies and similar technologies from Google Analytics and Meta (Facebook) Pixel to measure site usage and advertising performance. You can control cookies through your browser settings; blocking them won't affect your ability to book a rental, though it may limit some site features. You can change your cookie choice any time with the Cookie settings link in the site footer.

## Third-party services

Booking is handled through Acuity Scheduling, a third-party scheduling platform; their own privacy policy governs how they handle the information you submit there. Email and text sign-ups are stored and sent through Sender. Analytics and advertising data pass through Google and Meta under their respective privacy policies.

## Questions about your data

If you have questions about the information we collect or want it removed, email [info@ennisslingshot.com](mailto:info@ennisslingshot.com) and we'll respond as quickly as we can.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our tools or practices change. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent update.
