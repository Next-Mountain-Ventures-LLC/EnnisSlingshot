## What's happening in Ennis, TX

Ennis has a handful of anchor events that shape its calendar year: the Bluebonnet Trails Festival in April, the National Polka Festival on Memorial Day weekend, and Texas Motorplex race weekends, which peak in March and October. The calendar below shows current and upcoming dates. For more ideas, see our full list of [things to do in Ennis](/ennis/).

## How to use this calendar

This calendar is updated as new dates are confirmed. If you're planning a trip around a specific event, double-check the organizer's site closer to your travel dates, since dates can shift from year to year.

## The recurring anchors

- **Ennis Bluebonnet Trails** — open every April 1–30; the [Bluebonnet Trails & Festival guide](/bluebonnets/) has the full seasonal breakdown.
- **Ennis Bluebonnet Trails Festival** — expected April 17–19, 2027 (dates subject to change — confirm on [bluebonnettrail.org](https://bluebonnettrail.org/) before you travel); see our [Bluebonnet Festival guide](/bluebonnets/festival/).
- **National Polka Festival** — every Memorial Day weekend; see our [festival guide](/ennis/national-polka-festival/).
- **Texas Motorplex race weekends** — season runs roughly February–November, with the NHRA Fall Nationals in October; see our [race weekend guide](/ennis/texas-motorplex/).
- **Downtown Farmers Market** — seasonal, April through October.
- **Blues on Main** — downtown blues festival, recently held in early September.
