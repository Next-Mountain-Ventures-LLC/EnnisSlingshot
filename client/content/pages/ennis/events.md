## What's happening in Ennis, TX

Ennis has a handful of anchor events that shape its calendar year: the Bluebonnet Trails Festival in April, the National Polka Festival on Memorial Day weekend, and Texas Motorplex race weekends, which peak in March and October. The list below shows current and upcoming dates as they're confirmed. This page is part of our [Things to Do in Ennis hub](/ennis/).

## How to use this calendar

The calendar below pulls from our current event listings and updates as new dates are confirmed. If you're planning a trip around a specific event, check back closer to your travel dates — some Ennis events, including the Bluebonnet Trails Festival, don't get official dates until a few months out.

## The recurring anchors

- **Ennis Bluebonnet Trails** — open every April 1–30; the [Bluebonnet Trails & Festival guide](/bluebonnets/) has the full seasonal breakdown.
- **Ennis Bluebonnet Trails Festival** — typically mid-April, historically the second or third weekend.
- **National Polka Festival** — every Memorial Day weekend; see our [festival guide](/ennis/national-polka-festival/).
- **Texas Motorplex race weekends** — season runs roughly February–November, with the NHRA Fall Nationals in October; see our [race weekend guide](/ennis/texas-motorplex/).
- **Downtown Farmers Market** — seasonal, April through October.
- **Blues on Main** — the second Saturday in June.
