## The short version

Ennis is 35 miles south of downtown Dallas on I-45 — about a 37-minute drive door to door, no highway changes. That makes it one of the easiest genuine day trips out of DFW: close enough to leave after breakfast and be home for dinner, far enough to feel like you actually went somewhere. This page is part of our [Things to Do in Ennis hub](/ennis/); below are three hour-by-hour itineraries depending on who's in the car.

## Getting there

Take I-45 South from downtown Dallas. It's a direct shot — no highway changes, no tricky exits — and the drive itself becomes part of the trip in April, when the shoulders and side roads start showing bluebonnets before you even reach Ennis. Traffic near the I-20 interchange can add 5–10 minutes on weekend mornings, especially during the Bluebonnet Trails Festival. Give yourself a little extra buffer on festival weekends and expect downtown parking to fill by mid-morning.

| From | Distance | Drive time |
|---|---|---|
| Downtown Dallas | 35 miles | ~37 minutes |
| Arlington | 52 miles | ~53 minutes |
| Fort Worth | 57 miles | ~59 minutes |

## Itinerary 1: The date day

For couples wanting a scenic, unhurried day built around the drive itself and a slower afternoon.

- **9:00 am** — Leave Dallas, arrive Ennis by ~9:40.
- **9:45 am** — Stop at the Ennis Welcome Center, 201 NW Main St, for a current trail map and current bloom status if you're visiting in April.
- **10:00 am – 12:00 pm** — Drive the Bluebonnet Trails at your own pace, or [book a 2-hour Slingshot experience](/slingshot-rental/bluebonnet-trail-experience/) and let someone else worry about the route — an open-cockpit drive through the wildflowers is a genuinely different kind of date than dinner and a movie.
- **12:15 pm** — Lunch downtown. See [Where to Eat](/ennis/where-to-eat/) for options.
- **1:30 pm** — Walk historic [Downtown Ennis](/ennis/downtown/) — Dallas Street's shops and the Railroad & Cultural Heritage Museum are both an easy stroll from lunch.
- **3:00 pm** — Sugar Ridge Winery in Bristol (on the north trail loop) for a tasting on the patio, weekends only.
- **5:00 pm** — Head back to Dallas, or extend the day with dinner in Waxahachie on the way home.

## Itinerary 2: The family day

For families with kids who want a mix of outdoors, easy food, and something visual to remember.

- **9:00 am** — Leave Dallas.
- **9:45 am** — Ennis Welcome Center for a trail map and orientation.
- **10:00 am – 11:30 am** — Meadow View Nature Area or Bluebonnet Park for photos and open space to run around — both are April-season bluebonnet spots with room for kids to explore.
- **11:45 am** — Lunch downtown (see [Where to Eat](/ennis/where-to-eat/) for family-friendly picks).
- **1:00 pm** — Ennis Railroad & Cultural Heritage Museum — compact and kid-friendly, especially for train-curious younger visitors.
- **2:30 pm** — Lake Bardwell for a walk, picnic, or a last stretch of outdoor time before the drive home.
- **4:00 pm** — Back on I-45 to Dallas.

## Itinerary 3: The car-enthusiast day

For visitors whose day in Ennis is really about the driving, the roads, and the motorsports scene.

- **9:00 am** — Leave Dallas early to beat weekend traffic.
- **9:40 am** — Arrive Ennis; check current happenings on the [Events Calendar](/ennis/events/) — if it's a race weekend, Texas Motorplex changes the whole plan.
- **10:00 am – 12:00 pm** — [Drive a Polaris Slingshot](/slingshot-rental/drive-and-go/) on the Bluebonnet Trails — an hour or two behind the wheel of an open-cockpit three-wheeler on real backroads, not a parking lot. See [Texas Motorplex](/ennis/texas-motorplex/) for what else is happening if there's a race on.
- **12:30 pm** — Lunch downtown.
- **1:30 pm** — Texas Motorplex, if it's a race weekend (season runs roughly February–November, with the NHRA Fall Nationals in October) — otherwise, Big O Speedway's dirt-track schedule (March–October) is worth checking.
- **4:00 pm** — Drive back to Dallas via I-45, or loop through Waxahachie if you want more road time on the way home.

## FAQ

**How far is Ennis, TX from Dallas?**
Ennis is about 35 miles south of downtown Dallas on I-45, roughly a 37-minute drive with normal traffic. It's one of the closer bluebonnet and day-trip destinations to the DFW metro.

**What's the best route from Dallas to Ennis?**
I-45 South, direct, no highway changes. From downtown Dallas it's about 35 miles; expect closer to 40–45 minutes if you're leaving from north Dallas suburbs or hit weekend traffic near I-20.

**Can you do Ennis as a day trip, or do you need to stay overnight?**
A day trip works well — most of what Ennis offers (the trails, downtown, a meal, one or two attractions) fits comfortably into 5–7 hours including drive time. Staying over makes sense mainly around the Bluebonnet Festival weekend or if you're pairing it with Waxahachie. See [Where to Stay](/ennis/where-to-stay/) for options.
