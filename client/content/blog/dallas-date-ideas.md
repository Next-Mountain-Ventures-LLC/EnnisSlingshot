Dates in Dallas, sorted by vibe and by how far you're willing to drive — romantic, adventurous, cheap, unique — with one 35-minute detour south that most Dallas date lists have never heard of.

## How We Ranked These

Every entry here gets scored on vibe (what kind of date it is), rough cost, how long it takes, and drive time from downtown Dallas. That's it — no invented star ratings, just enough to help you filter fast.

## Romantic Dates

Klyde Warren Park at golden hour, a walk through the Dallas Arboretum, dinner in Bishop Arts, the Nasher Sculpture Center's garden, or the observation deck at Reunion Tower — all classic Dallas romantic picks that hold up because they're genuinely nice, not because a listicle says so.

## Adventurous Dates

## Cheap Dates Under $20

A picnic at White Rock Lake, a free evening at the Trinity Groves murals, browsing the Dallas Farmers Market, or a walk down the Katy Trail cost close to nothing and still make for a genuinely good date.

## The Bluebonnet Season Exception: A 35-Minute Drive South

Every list above skips this one because it doesn't exist most of the year. Ennis, Texas sits about 35 miles — roughly 37 minutes — south of downtown Dallas on I-45, and every April its back roads fill with bluebonnets. During that window, and only that window, we run the Golden Hour Date Night: a two-seat Slingshot ride timed to catch the fields at golden hour, $169 for the two of you.

It's honestly not a year-round pick — it's an April pick. If you're reading this outside bluebonnet season, bookmark it. If you're reading this in April, it's the one entry on this whole list nobody else in Dallas date content is telling you about.
