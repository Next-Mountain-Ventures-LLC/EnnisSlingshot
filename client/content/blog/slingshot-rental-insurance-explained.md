Every Ennis Slingshot rental includes comprehensive insurance — no deposit, a $500 maximum out-of-pocket if something goes wrong, and approval that typically clears within 24 hours. Here's exactly how it works.

## What's Actually Included

Comprehensive insurance comes standard on every rental — Drive & Go, Bluebonnet Trail Experience, or Golden Hour Date Night. No deposit is charged up front, and no separate damage waiver purchase is required at booking.

## What Happens If Something Goes Wrong

If damage does happen during your rental, your maximum out-of-pocket exposure is capped at $500 — not the full repair cost of the vehicle. That figure is fixed regardless of what the actual damage turns out to be.

## The Driver Approval Process

Booking requires a driver approval step before your rental is confirmed, which typically clears within 24 hours. This isn't a formality we skip — it's part of how we keep the no-deposit model workable.

## How This Compares to the Category

Refundable damage deposits in the $150–$500+ range are standard practice among Slingshot rental operators generally. We built our model specifically to avoid that upfront cash-on-the-table friction.

|  | Ennis Slingshot | Category Norm |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deposit | $0 | $150–$500+ |
| Max out-of-pocket if damaged | $500 | Varies, often the full deposit |
| Approval time | ~24 hours | Varies by operator |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is insurance included when you rent a Slingshot?

Yes, on every Ennis Slingshot rental — comprehensive coverage, no deposit, $500 max out-of-pocket.

### Do I need a deposit to rent a Slingshot?

Not with us — no deposit is charged at booking. Many operators in the category do require one, typically $150–$500+.
