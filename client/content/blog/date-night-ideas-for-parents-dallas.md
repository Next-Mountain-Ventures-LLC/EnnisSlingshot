A sitter window is a hard deadline, not a suggestion. Here's a list organized by exactly how much time you actually have — 3, 4, or 6 hours, door-to-door.

## 3-Hour Window Dates

Quick, close-to-home options: coffee and a walk, an afternoon matinee, or a shorter tasting menu — all comfortably done and home inside three hours.

## 4-Hour Window Dates

A bit more room to work with: a longer dinner, a paint-and-sip class, or a museum visit all fit comfortably in four hours.

## 6-Hour Window Dates (Full Morning to Afternoon)

This is where an Ennis day trip actually becomes realistic. Leave by 9am, ride the 2-Hour Bluebonnet Trail Experience at 10am (orientation included), grab lunch downtown Ennis, and you're home by 2-3pm. From Frisco or Plano, budget close to an hour each way; from Uptown Dallas, closer to 40 minutes. This is honestly April-only — the trails run April 1-30 — so this section is for parents reading in bluebonnet season, or planning ahead for next spring.

## Saturday Morning Dates (While the Kids Are at Practice or Camp)

A farmers market walk or a short scenic drive both work well in that in-between morning window most "date night" content ignores entirely.

## Evening Dates That Respect a Sitter's Bedtime

An early dinner reservation or a sunset patio both get you home at a reasonable hour without cutting the evening short mid-conversation.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What can parents do for a date night in Dallas?

Depends on your sitter window — see the 3-, 4-, and 6-hour sections above.

### What is a good short date night?

Coffee and a walk, or a matinee — both fit comfortably inside a 3-hour window.
