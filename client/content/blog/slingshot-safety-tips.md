These are the things I actually want you thinking about before you touch the pedals — not a generic "drive safe" list assembled from other sites, but the stuff that matters specifically in a Slingshot.

1. **Buckle up and check the fit.** Three-point belt, snug — there are no doors holding you in, so this matters more than it does in a car.
2. **Adjust your mirrors before you move.** Side mirrors only, no traditional rearview.
3. **Leave more following distance than you would in a car.** Renters have described Slingshot braking as feeling more like a motorcycle's than a car's — give yourself the extra room.
4. **Remember the rear wheel tracks inside the front wheels on tight turns.** Reverse-trike geometry behaves differently than a standard car through a corner — it's not a problem, just something to be aware of.
5. **Don't fight the AutoDrive transmission.** It downshifts on its own when you lift off the throttle, and it'll feel like engine braking. Let it do its job.
6. **Sunscreen, sunglasses, hydration.** Open cockpit, Texas April sun — you're exposed the whole ride.
7. **Know where the trail map lives.** Glove box, every rental, with our curated routes marked.
8. **Watch for loose gravel and wet leaves on the rural stretches.** These are working back roads, not manicured park trails.
9. **Keep both hands on the wheel through the test loop.** That short supervised loop before the real ride exists for a reason — use it.
10. **Ask before you're unsure.** Staff are there the entire time. If something feels off, say so before you're out on the trail.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is a Polaris Slingshot safe?

It's generally considered safer than a motorcycle and less safe than an enclosed car — see our full [safety equipment breakdown](https://ennisslingshot.com/blog/polaris-slingshot-safety-features/) for the complete inventory.

### What should a first-time Slingshot driver know?

The 10 tips above cover it — belts, following distance, sun protection, and asking questions before you're unsure.

### How do you stay safe in a Slingshot?

Belt on, extra following distance, sun protection, and don't skip the test loop.
