Closed-toe shoes, layers you can adjust, sunglasses and sunscreen, and a helmet — which we provide regardless of Texas's conditional exemption. Here's the full practical list.

## The Non-Negotiables

Closed-toe shoes for real pedal control — no flip-flops or sandals. Sunglasses or eye protection, since there's no windshield above the dash in an open cockpit. Sunscreen, every time.

## Do You Need a Helmet?

The legal exemption is conditional — 21+ requires a training course or qualifying health insurance, not just age. We provide a helmet to every renter regardless of that exemption, and offer a $25 Bluetooth communication helmet add-on so driver and passenger can actually talk. See our [full helmet-law breakdown](https://ennisslingshot.com/blog/do-i-need-a-helmet-for-a-polaris-slingshot/) for the statute details.

## What to Wear in April (Our Actual Season)

April in Ennis averages a 78°F high and 55°F low — layer for a cool morning start that warms up fast. A light jacket you can remove and stash in the storage compartment works well. With roughly a 26% chance of rain on any given day, a light rain layer is a smart add — see our [rain-driving guide](https://ennisslingshot.com/blog/polaris-slingshot-in-the-rain/) for more.

## Can You Wear Shorts in a Slingshot?

Yes — no engine-heat exposure risk the way a motorcycle might have. Just plan for sun exposure on bare legs during a 1–2 hour ride and bring sunscreen.

## What to Leave at Home (or Stash in Storage)

Loose scarves or hats that can blow off at speed, open-toe sandals, and anything valuable that isn't secured. The sealed storage compartment is there for phones and wallets.

## If You're Coming for Bluebonnet Photos Too

What looks good in photos and what's practical to drive in aren't always the same thing. See our dedicated [bluebonnet photo styling guide](https://ennisslingshot.com/blog/what-to-wear-for-bluebonnet-photos-outfit-ideas-that-wont-fight-the-flowers/) for the aesthetic side.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need a helmet for a Polaris Slingshot?

Legally conditional for 21+; we provide one to every renter regardless.

### What should I wear on a Slingshot ride?

Closed-toe shoes, eye protection, sunscreen, and weather-appropriate layers.

### Can you wear shorts in a Slingshot?

Yes, just plan for sun exposure.
