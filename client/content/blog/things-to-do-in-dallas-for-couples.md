Four tiers — active, creative, scenic, indulgent — each with an honest verdict on whether it actually works as a couples activity, not just a tourist checklist with "for couples" bolted onto the title.

## Active Couples Activities

A climbing gym session, a bike ride down the Katy Trail, or a kayak rental on White Rock Lake all work well for a couple who wants to move rather than sit.

## Creative Couples Activities

A pottery studio, a paint-and-sip night, or a couples cooking class all give you something to talk about besides each other for an hour, which somehow makes the conversation better.

## Scenic Couples Activities

A rooftop view or a Klyde Warren Park picnic both work anytime. The standout entry, though, is the one I'll be honest about scoping correctly: the Ennis Bluebonnet Trails Slingshot ride, $79 solo or $149 two-up, about 35 minutes south of Dallas. Verdict: the most memorable entry on this whole list — but it's April-only, so plan ahead rather than expecting it on a random weekend.

## Indulgent Couples Activities

A tasting menu, a spa day, or a wine flight all earn their place when you want to treat yourselves rather than DIY the date.
