The Houston-to-Dallas run on I-45 is about 240 miles, roughly 4 hours straight through. Here's where it's actually worth stopping — and one stop, close to the Dallas end, I'd genuinely plan the trip around rather than just pass through.

## The Route at a Glance

I-45 is the default corridor for a reason — it's the direct shot. Budget the full 4 hours plus stops; nobody does this drive in one uninterrupted sitting comfortably.

## Leaving Houston

Huntsville sits roughly at the midpoint of the corridor and is home to the "A Tribute to Courage" Sam Houston statue and a visitor center — confirm current hours before planning a stop there specifically.

## The Midpoint Stretch

Honestly, the Madisonville/Buffalo/Fairfield stretch is mostly gas-and-food territory — I won't oversell it. Good for a bathroom break, not a destination.

## Corsicana

About 18 miles before Ennis, Corsicana is home to the century-old Collin Street Bakery — confirm current hours at collinstreetbakery.com before building a stop around it.

## Ennis: The Stop Worth Planning Around

This is the one I'm biased about, and I'll own that. If you're driving through in April, the Ennis Bluebonnet Trails — 40-plus miles of Ennis Garden Club-mapped routes — are worth breaking your trip for entirely, not just a bathroom stop. Swing by the Welcome Center (201 NW Main St), and if you've got an hour to spare, the $69.99 Drive & Go is a genuinely different way to break up a long highway drive than another gas station.

## Rolling Into Dallas

From Ennis, it's about 35 miles / 37 minutes into downtown Dallas — the easiest final stretch of the whole drive.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is there to do between Houston and Dallas?

Huntsville's Sam Houston statue, Corsicana's Collin Street Bakery, and Ennis (especially in April) are the real stops — the middle stretch is mostly gas and food.

### Where should I stop on I-45 between Houston and Dallas?

Ennis, about 35 minutes before Dallas, is the stop I'd actually build time around — especially during bluebonnet season.
