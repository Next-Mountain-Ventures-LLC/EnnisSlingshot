I live ten minutes from Texas Motorplex. I still don't know the exact exit number off the top of my head — I just drive it. That's the problem with most "how to get to the track" content online: it's written by someone who's never actually made the drive, and it's guessing at the same three facts everyone else is guessing at. I'm not going to add to that pile. Here's what I actually know, and where I'm sending you to confirm the rest before you go.

## Where Texas Motorplex Actually Is

Ennis sits within about 30 minutes of I-20, I-30, I-35 and I-45 — that's not marketing copy, that's the Ennis Economic Development Corporation's own regional-access pitch, and it happens to be true for getting to the track too. Most people coming from Dallas will take I-45 south. If you're coming from somewhere else in the metroplex, you likely have a more direct route than you'd think.

## The Two Main Ways In

I'm not going to hand you a specific exit number I haven't personally double-checked against a live map on the day you're reading this — exits get renumbered, construction reroutes traffic, and a wrong exit number in a blog post is worse than no exit number at all. Pull up Texas Motorplex's address directly on your maps app the day you drive out; it'll route you correctly off either I-45 or the US-287 approach.

## Parking — What to Expect

I don't have a confirmed, current parking price to give you, and I'd rather tell you that plainly than make one up. What I can tell you: on the marquee days (Friday night qualifying, Sunday eliminations), the lots fill and the roads around the track get genuinely congested. Give yourself more runway than you think you need.

## When to Arrive

Same story on gate times — they shift year to year and I'm not going to guess. Check texasmotorplex.com or the Stampede of Speed schedule for the exact times before your specific race day, and plan to arrive earlier than that posted time on Friday and Sunday specifically.

## The Downtown Ennis Alternative

Here's an idea most out-of-town race guides never mention because they don't know the town exists beyond the track: eat downtown first. Ennis has a real, walkable downtown a few minutes from the Motorplex. Grab food, let the worst of the pre-race traffic clear, then head to the gate. Whether a shuttle exists on any given event weekend is something I'd confirm with the track directly rather than assume — but the "don't drive straight into peak arrival traffic" logic holds regardless.

## Why October Is the Weekend to Plan Ahead For

Search interest in "Texas Motorplex parking" itself jumps to roughly 260 a month in October — that's people planning ahead of the NHRA Fall Nationals, and it's a reasonable signal to do the same. I'm not going to tell you it always sells out; I don't have that data. I will tell you that waiting until the week of is a worse bet than booking and planning early.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much is parking at Texas Motorplex?

I don't have a current, confirmed price to give you — check texasmotorplex.com directly for this year's rate.

### What exit is Texas Motorplex off I-45?

Pull the address up on your maps app the day you drive — exit numbers change, and I'd rather send you to a live route than a number that might be stale by the time you read this.

### How early should I get to Texas Motorplex?

Earlier than the posted gate time on Friday night and Sunday specifically — those are the two days that actually fill up.
