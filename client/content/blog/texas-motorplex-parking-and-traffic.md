I live ten minutes from Texas Motorplex. I still don't know the exact exit number off the top of my head — I just drive it. Here's what I actually know, and where I'm sending you to confirm the rest before you go.

## Where Texas Motorplex Actually Is

Ennis sits within about 30 minutes of I-20, I-30, I-35 and I-45, which works in your favor for getting to the track too. Most people coming from Dallas will take I-45 south. If you're coming from somewhere else in the metroplex, you likely have a more direct route than you'd think.

## The Two Main Ways In

Exits get renumbered and construction reroutes traffic, so skip the printed exit number. Pull up Texas Motorplex's address directly on your maps app the day you drive out; it'll route you correctly off either I-45 or the US-287 approach.

## Parking — What to Expect

Check texasmotorplex.com for current parking prices before you go. What I can tell you: on the marquee days (Friday night qualifying, Sunday eliminations), the lots fill and the roads around the track get genuinely congested. Give yourself more runway than you think you need.

## When to Arrive

Gate times shift year to year, so check texasmotorplex.com or the Stampede of Speed schedule for the exact times before your specific race day, and plan to arrive earlier than that posted time on Friday and Sunday specifically.

## The Downtown Ennis Alternative

Here's an idea most out-of-town race guides never mention because they don't know the town exists beyond the track: eat downtown first. Ennis has a real, walkable downtown a few minutes from the Motorplex. Grab food, let the worst of the pre-race traffic clear, then head to the gate. If you're hoping for a shuttle, check with the track for your event weekend — but the "don't drive straight into peak arrival traffic" logic holds regardless.

## Why October Is the Weekend to Plan Ahead For

Plenty of race fans start planning well ahead of the NHRA Fall Nationals in October, and it's smart to do the same. Waiting until the week of is a worse bet than booking and planning early.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much is parking at Texas Motorplex?

Check texasmotorplex.com directly for this year's parking rate.

### What exit is Texas Motorplex off I-45?

Pull the address up on your maps app the day you drive — exit numbers change, and I'd rather send you to a live route than a number that might be stale by the time you read this.

### How early should I get to Texas Motorplex?

Earlier than the posted gate time on Friday night and Sunday specifically — those are the two days that actually fill up.
