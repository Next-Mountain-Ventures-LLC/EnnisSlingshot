A real Fort Worth list — not a Dallas list with the city name swapped — plus one honest day-trip pitch to Ennis.

## Culture and Art

The Kimbell Art Museum, the Fort Worth Water Gardens (free, iconic, genuinely photogenic at night), and the Fort Worth Botanic Garden all deliver a real culture-forward date.

## Classic Fort Worth

The Fort Worth Stockyards (rodeo nights, live music), Magnolia Avenue's walkable restaurant and bar strip, and Sundance Square all deliver the identity Fort Worth dates that don't exist in Dallas.

## Something Different

Coyote Drive-In along the river, a Trinity River sunset paddle, or a distillery or brewery tour all break from the standard dinner-and-drinks routine.

## Food-First Dates

A tasting-menu splurge, a food-hall crawl, or a coffee-and-dessert walk down Magnolia all put dining at the center of the date.

## Seasonal and Outdoor

A Trinity Trails bike date, a rooftop patio, or a seasonal event all work depending on the time of year — check current dates for anything time-specific.

## Is Ennis Worth the Drive From Fort Worth?

Honestly: about 57 miles, roughly 59 minutes, via US-287 and I-45. That's not a spontaneous Tuesday-night drive — it's a planned special-occasion one. If you're booking a late-afternoon Slingshot slot in April for golden-hour light, leave Fort Worth with a full hour of buffer.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are good date ideas in Fort Worth?

The Kimbell, the Water Gardens, the Stockyards, or Magnolia Avenue all deliver a genuine Fort Worth date — see the categories above.

### What can couples do in Fort Worth at night?

The Water Gardens after dark, a Stockyards rodeo night, or Magnolia Avenue's restaurant strip.
