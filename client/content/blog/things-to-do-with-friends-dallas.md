"Things to do with friends" lists are usually couples content with the word swapped in. This one's actually built around the group-of-3-or-4 question: can we all do it together, and what does it cost per person?

## Active Friend-Group Ideas

Axe throwing and climbing gyms both scale naturally to a group of 3–4. And if it's April: our fleet runs two Slingshots, so a group of 4 rides together in one run — $149 for the 2-hour Bluebonnet Trail Experience per vehicle (two riders), meaning a group of 4 splits into two vehicles at $149 each, roughly $74.50 a person.

## Chill Friend-Group Ideas

A park picnic, a coffee crawl, or a game cafe all work when nobody wants a high-energy day.

## Boozy Friend-Group Ideas

A Deep Ellum brewery crawl or a wine-flight night both split evenly and don't require reservations for exactly four.

## Budget-Friendly Friend-Group Ideas

Free museum days, a park hangout, or a potluck night in — zero pressure to spend to have a good day together.
