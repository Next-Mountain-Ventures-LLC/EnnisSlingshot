Polaris revealed the Slingshot in 2014 and launched it in 2015. The 2020 model year is the real dividing line — new engine, new transmission — and everything since has been trim expansion on that foundation.

## 2014 — The Reveal

Polaris unveiled the Slingshot as a new category entirely — a three-wheeled autocycle rather than a car or a motorcycle.

## 2015 — Launch, Including in Texas

The Slingshot reached the market in 2015. That same year, Motorcycle.com covered its legalization in Texas specifically — the beginning of the autocycle classification that makes the current Ennis business model possible. See our [Texas Slingshot laws guide](https://ennisslingshot.com/blog/texas-slingshot-laws/) for the full legal picture.

## 2015–2019 — The GM 2.4L Ecotec Era

Early Slingshots ran a GM 2.4L Ecotec engine putting out 173 hp. If you're shopping used listings from this era, know you're looking at a meaningfully different powertrain than the current lineup.

## 2020 — The Redesign That Changed Everything

Polaris replaced the Ecotec with its own ProStar 2.0L inline-four and launched AutoDrive, a hydraulically actuated automated 5-speed — no clutch, no manual shifting. Per Polaris's own reporting, the company nearly doubled its May–September business after AutoDrive launched. This is the single biggest turning point in Slingshot history.

## 2021–2025 — Trim Expansion

The lineup grew from there — S, SL, SLR, R, Signature Edition, and Grand Touring trims all built on the 2020 ProStar/AutoDrive foundation.

## 2023 — Texas Law Catches Up

SB 1115 amended the Texas autocycle helmet statute (Tex. Transp. Code §661.0015), effective September 1, 2023. See our [full Texas laws breakdown](https://ennisslingshot.com/blog/texas-slingshot-laws/) for what changed.

## 2026 — Where the Lineup Stands Today

See our [full 2026 spec table](https://ennisslingshot.com/blog/polaris-slingshot-specs/) for current trim-by-trim MSRP and horsepower.

## So, What Year Is the Best Slingshot to Buy?

2020 or newer is the practical line — that's when you get the ProStar engine and AutoDrive together. This is general guidance on what changed and why it matters, not personalized purchase advice.
