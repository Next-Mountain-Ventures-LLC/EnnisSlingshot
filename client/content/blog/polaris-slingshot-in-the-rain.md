Yes, you can drive a Slingshot in the rain. It's not waterproof — but the interior is water-resistant and storage is sealed, and we operate rain or shine unless conditions turn unsafe. Rescheduling is always free.

## Does the Polaris Slingshot Have a Roof?

No roof or doors by default — open cockpit by design. An optional Slingshade roof exists as an accessory on some trims, but our rental fleet is open-cockpit.

## Can a Polaris Slingshot Get Wet?

Honestly, yes — you and the seats will get wet in real rain. The interior uses water-resistant materials and storage compartments are gasketed, which protects your belongings, but this is not a waterproof vehicle.

## What It's Actually Like to Drive in the Rain

Car Talk's Jim Motavalli found it "surprisingly comfortable" with proper rain gear, noting front-tire spray angles away from the cockpit rather than into it. Slingshot Forums owners report some visor fogging and streaking in sustained rain — manageable, but worth knowing about ahead of time.

## Hydroplaning and Handling Caution

Forum owners note the Slingshot's light rear end can be more prone to hydroplaning in standing water than a heavier car — general driving-physics caution, not a documented incident rate. Reduce speed and avoid standing water where you can, same as you would in any lightweight vehicle.

## What Happens If It Rains During Your Booked Ride?

We operate rain or shine unless conditions are genuinely unsafe — that call gets made the morning of, checked against real conditions. If weather (or a mechanical issue) does stop your ride, you get a full, free reschedule. No refund, but no cost to move your date either.

## Ennis, Texas April Weather — What to Actually Expect

April in Ennis averages a high of 78°F, a low of 55°F, and roughly a 26% chance of rain on any given day — meaning most days are dry, but it's worth planning for the possibility either way. These are historical averages, not a forecast for your specific date.

## What to Bring Just in Case

A light rain layer, sunglasses (glare comes back fast after rain), and keep your phone and valuables in the sealed storage compartment.
