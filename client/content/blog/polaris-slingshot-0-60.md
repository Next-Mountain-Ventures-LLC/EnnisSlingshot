The Slingshot R goes 0-60 in about 4.9 seconds. Other trims are slower, though Polaris hasn't published a separate figure for each one. Here's the sourced number, and what it actually feels like with no doors.

## How Fast Is a Polaris Slingshot 0-60?

The R trim runs 0-60 in roughly 4.9 seconds, per Motorcycle.com's first-ride review. Lower-horsepower trims (S, SL) are slower, but the manufacturer hasn't published an independent 0-60 figure for every trim, so we won't invent one here.

## Is a Polaris Slingshot Fast?

Sub-5-second 0-60 puts the R trim ahead of most everyday sedans. Combined with an open cockpit and low seating position, it reads as considerably quicker than the number alone suggests.

## How Much Horsepower Does a Slingshot R Have?

204.1 hp @ 7,500 rpm and 149.8 lb-ft @ 6,500 rpm. Across the 2026 lineup, S and SL trims run 180 hp, while SLR, R, Signature, and Grand Touring trims run 204 hp.

## The Older Ecotec Era, for Context

If you've read older reviews quoting different numbers, they're describing a different engine. MY2015–2019 Slingshots ran a GM 2.4L Ecotec engine at 173 hp. The current ProStar 2.0L inline-four, introduced for MY2020 alongside the AutoDrive transmission, is what every current-lineup figure refers to.

## What 4.9 Seconds Feels Like With No Doors

An open cockpit and a seating position lower than most cars changes how acceleration registers — wind, engine vibration, and no door panel between you and the road all make the number feel more dramatic than it would in an enclosed car. That's exactly why every orientation I run covers throttle discipline specifically — first-timers consistently underestimate how quick it feels until they're actually in the seat.
