This isn't another undated "best of Dallas" list. It's a checklist — organized by category, each entry flagged honestly as a local favorite or a tourist-magnet, so you know what you're actually walking into.

## Iconic Dallas Landmarks

Reunion Tower, the Sixth Floor Museum, and the Dallas Arts District anchor the must-see list — verify current hours and admission before you go, since these details shift.

## Food & Drink Essentials

Deep Ellum and the Bishop Arts District both deliver a genuine Dallas food-and-drink crawl — specific restaurant names turn over fast enough that I won't lock in a list here; check current standouts before you plan a night around one.

## Arts, Culture & Museums

The Dallas Museum of Art and the Perot Museum round out a full culture day — both worth checking for a free-admission day before you go.

## Outdoor & Parks

Klyde Warren Park and the Dallas Arboretum are the standing outdoor picks, good any season.

## Only-in-Dallas Experiences

The Bishop Arts District's specific character and the Dallas Arts District's concentration of venues are both genuinely Dallas-specific rather than generic big-city fare.

## Nightlife & Entertainment

Deep Ellum again, this time after dark — live music venues within walking distance of each other.

## Within 45 Minutes

This is the section that actually earns its place on a couples' bucket list rather than a generic Dallas rundown. Ennis, about 35 minutes south on I-45, is home to the Bluebonnet Trails every April — and my own 2-hour Slingshot ride through them. Waxahachie's historic downtown makes a good pairing on the same trip.
