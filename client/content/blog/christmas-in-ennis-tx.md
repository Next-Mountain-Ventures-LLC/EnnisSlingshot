Ennis runs its own downtown Christmas parade and block party every December — no Dallas-sized crowds, no paid parking, just historic downtown decorated for the season.

## Does Ennis Have a Christmas Parade?

Yes — the Lights of Ennis Parade & Block Party, hosted by the Ennis Chamber of Commerce, held annually the first Thursday of December in the illuminated historic downtown district. It includes decorated storefronts, live entertainment, food trucks, Santa Claus, and a nativity display. Santa appears at the Ennis Railroad and Cultural Heritage Museum, with coffee, cookies, and hot chocolate served in the museum's caboose. Confirm the exact 2026 date and schedule at ennischamber.com before you go, since the Chamber sets it year to year.

## Downtown Ennis Holiday Atmosphere

Historic downtown Ennis — the same brick streets and Dallas Street storefronts that anchor bluebonnet season — gets decorated for the parade and stays festive through December. It's a genuinely walkable few blocks, easy to pair with dinner downtown.

## Nearby Ellis County Towns

Midlothian, Red Oak, and Italy each run their own small-town December events most years, but we don't have independently verified 2026 dates or event names for any of the three at publish time — check each town's own city or chamber site before planning a trip around a specific date.

## Why Small-Town Christmas Beats the Dallas Crowds

Free parking, a fraction of the crowd, and an evening you can actually walk through at your own pace — that's the honest trade-off small-town Ellis County holiday events offer against the major paid Dallas displays.
