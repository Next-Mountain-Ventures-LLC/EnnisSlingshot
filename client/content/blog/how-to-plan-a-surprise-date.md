Pick the date, book under the right name, block the calendar, manage the cover story, and brief the recipient just enough. Here's the full framework, including the one booking detail most surprise-planning advice misses entirely.

## Step 1: Pick a Date That Actually Works

Check your partner's real calendar without asking directly, and build in a buffer day — for an outdoor April activity, a weather reschedule is a real possibility, not a hypothetical.

## Step 2: Decide Who Books (and Whose Name Is on the Approval Email)

This is the part most surprise-planning advice skips. After booking, we email a verification link, and driver approval usually clears within 24 hours. If your partner is going to be the driver, that email could land on their phone before the reveal. Two workarounds: book far enough ahead that a "pending approval" email doesn't read as unusual, or book yourself as the driver so your partner rides as the passenger for the reveal — they can drive on a future visit.

## Step 3: Block the Calendar Without Tipping Your Hand

Use a vague calendar label, ask for the day off separately from mentioning any plan, and line up a friend or family member who can "need a favor" that day if you need real cover.

## Step 4: Build a Believable Cover Story

Keep it simple and in the same general direction and time window as the real plan — "lunch in Waxahachie" covers a late change of plans without raising suspicion.

## Step 5: Pack for Them Without Giving It Away

For an open-cockpit April drive: sunglasses, closed-toe shoes, a phone mount if they'll want photos, sunscreen. Stash the bag somewhere they won't notice you packing it.

## Step 6: The Reveal and the Reschedule Backup Plan

Arrive at the Ennis Welcome Center about 15 minutes early. If weather forces a change, free rescheduling means the backup plan is simply "we'll go next weekend instead" — which takes most of the stress out of an outdoor surprise.

## Worked Example: Surprising a Partner With an April Slingshot Ride

Buy a gift card in December. In March, start watching the calendar for an April weekend. Book the ride two to three weeks out under your own name if you're the driver. The week of, confirm the cover story and pack quietly. On the day, drive to the Welcome Center under the cover story, then reveal it.

## If You're Buying It as a Gift Instead of Planning the Date Yourself

A gift card removes most of the booking-logistics problem entirely — the recipient books their own date and handles their own approval. Often the simpler surprise route. Redemption is any operating day next April.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do you plan a surprise date?

Pick the date, sort out who books and whose name is on any approval email, block the calendar quietly, and have a simple cover story ready.

### How do you surprise your partner with an experience?

A gift card is often the lowest-logistics option — they book and handle their own approval, so the surprise is just in the giving.
