Somewhere between 8 and 9 in 10 people say they'd rather get an experience than a thing, depending on which survey you read. Here's the sourced, dated data.

## The Headline Number

A GetYourGuide survey (via Travel Agent Central, 2026) found 92% of Americans would rather receive an experiential gift than a physical one, up from 77% the prior year. A separate Tinggly survey put the figure at 82%. These are two different surveys with two different methodologies — not the same number stated twice.

## Why Experiences Beat Things, According to the Research

Morning Consult (June 2025) found three-quarters of respondents increased experiential spending because they perceive more value than from products, and more than half said they already have too many things. Square's research found 61% give experience gifts to create a "special memory" and 55% to give something "unique."

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do people prefer experiences or things?

Most survey data says experiences — 82-92% depending on the survey.

### Why are experiences better gifts?

Research points to perceived value, a "special memory," and giving something unique rather than one more thing.
