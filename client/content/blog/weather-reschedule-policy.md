We operate rain or shine unless conditions are genuinely unsafe. If weather does stop your ride, it's a free reschedule — no refund, but no cost to move your date either.

## Our Actual Policy, Word for Word

We operate rain or shine unless conditions are unsafe. Weather or mechanical issues mean no refund, but a full reschedule at no cost. Outside of a weather cancellation, free rescheduling is available up to 7 days before your date, subject to availability.

## What "Unsafe" Means in Practice

High wind, lightning, or standing water on the trail routes — the kind of conditions that would make any open-cockpit vehicle a genuine risk, not just an uncomfortable ride. That call gets made the morning of, against real conditions, not a forecast from days out.

## Does a Slingshot Have a Roof? What Getting Caught in Rain Is Actually Like

No roof or HVAC on our fleet — open cockpit by default. The interior uses water-resistant materials and gasketed storage, so you'll get wetter than in a car, but less than the average unprotected motorcyclist.

## How Rescheduling Works

Free up to 7 days before your date, subject to availability. A weather-related cancellation on the day itself always gets a free reschedule, regardless of that window.

## What to Do If You're Gifting a Ride and Worried About Weather

Tell the recipient plainly: the ride runs rain or shine unless it's unsafe, and a weather day just means picking a new date at no extra cost. That removes most of the anxiety around gifting an outdoor April activity.
