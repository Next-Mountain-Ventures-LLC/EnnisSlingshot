A double date is different from four friends hanging out — it needs to work as two couples, not just a group of four. Here's a list filtered specifically for that.

## What Makes a Double Date Work

Three failure modes to avoid: one couple bored while the other's engaged, an awkward split check, or an activity one person physically can't do. Every entry below is picked to sidestep at least one of those.

## Active & Competitive

Whirlyball, Topgolf, axe throwing, or go-karts all give two couples something to compete over rather than just talk at each other.

## Creative & Hands-On

A pottery class, a cooking class, or a paint-and-sip all split evenly across four and give everyone something to show for the night.

## Something Different

An escape room, a comedy show, or a drive-in movie all work well for two couples who want a shared experience without a ton of logistics.

## Day Trips & Drives: The Two-Slingshot Convoy

This is the entry built for exactly this occasion: two couples, two Slingshots, one convoy through the Ennis Bluebonnet Trails. Our fleet is two vehicles, so a double date fills it exactly — one driver and one rider per Slingshot, decide who drives ahead of time. It's honestly April-only, tied to bloom season, so this is a spring plan, not a this-weekend plan.

## Food-First Double Dates

A tasting menu or a food-hall crawl both work when the point of the night is the food, not an activity.
