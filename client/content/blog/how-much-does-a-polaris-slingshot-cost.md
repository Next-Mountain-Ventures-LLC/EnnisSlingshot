Source: MotoMember 2026 Slingshot Model Comparison.

## What Drives the Price Difference Between Trims?

Horsepower (180 vs. 204) is the baseline split, with higher trims adding features like the RIDE COMMAND display, Brembo brakes, paddle shift, and color-shifting paint. Full spec detail lives in our [2026 spec breakdown](https://ennisslingshot.com/blog/polaris-slingshot-specs/).

## The Real Per-Hour Math: Buying vs. Renting

A $34,999 R-trim Slingshot driven 20 hours a year works out to roughly $1,750 per hour in the first year alone, before financing, insurance, or maintenance. Our 2-hour experience costs $79–$149 flat, with insurance and orientation included. That math isn't an argument that buying is wrong — it depends entirely on how often you'd actually drive one — but it's the honest comparison a dealership page won't run.

## Are Polaris Slingshots Worth Buying?

Depends on how often you'd actually drive one. No independent resale-value or long-term ownership-satisfaction data exists to make a blanket claim either way. The lowest-risk way to find out before spending $25K+ is to try one first.
