A gift for two needs a different kind of detail than a solo gift: who actually books it, who pays, and does it expire. Every entry here answers all three.

## What Makes a Good "Together" Experience Gift

It has to require two people, not just allow two — a solo spa gift certificate for one isn't a couples gift just because you buy two of them.

## Under $50

A coffee tasting, a dessert-making class, or a small shared activity all work as a lower-stakes together gift.

## $50–$150: The Sweet Spot

This is where our own Driver + Rider Bluebonnet Trail Experience lands: $149, two hours, insurance included, no deposit. Who books it: either partner, but only the driver needs insurance approval. Redeem any operating day next April — it's genuinely seasonal, not year-round.

## $150+: Splurge Experiences

For an anniversary or wedding gift, a bigger multi-hour or overnight experience earns its higher price with a genuinely memorable scale.

## How to Give an Experience Gift Without Ruining the Surprise

## Gift Cards vs. Booking the Date Yourself
