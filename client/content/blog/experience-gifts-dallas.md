Sorted by price band, with a note on redeem windows — because the single most common complaint about experience gifts is finding out too late that yours had blackout dates.

## How We Picked These

Real, current prices — no "starting at" games — and a plain note on when each gift actually has to be redeemed by.

## $25–$50: Small but Memorable

A class credit, a tasting flight, or a mini workshop session all land in this range and rarely disappoint as a smaller gift.

## $50–$100: The Sweet Spot

Our own $69.99 1-Hour Drive & Go sits right in this range — insurance included, no deposit, no motorcycle license needed.

## $100–$200: Splurge-Worthy

The 2-Hour Bluebonnet Trail Experience lands here at $149 for two. One honest note: this redeems any April day next season, not immediately — gift cards are new for us this year, so plan the reveal around that timing.

## $200+: Go Big

Multi-day trips, VIP packages, and group experiences all live in this tier for the gift-giver who wants to go all-in.

## What to Know Before You Buy an Experience Gift

Always check the redeem window and any blackout dates before you buy. Ours is straightforward: any Slingshot gift redeems on any operating day next April — the trails run April 1-30 only, so that's the one date range to plan around.
