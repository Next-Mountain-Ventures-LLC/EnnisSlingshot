Nervous for the first five minutes, grinning by the first turn. That's the pattern I watch play out with almost every first-timer, and most of what feels "scary" beforehand turns out to be unfamiliarity, not actual difficulty.

## The Five Minutes Before You Drive

Expectation-setting is what actually reduces first-drive anxiety, more than anything I could say once you're behind the wheel. Orientation covers the pedals, the wheel, the mirrors, and AutoDrive basics before you ever sit down to drive. Want the full numbered walkthrough of that sequence? See our [step-by-step how-to-drive guide](https://ennisslingshot.com/blog/how-to-drive-a-polaris-slingshot/).

## Pulling Out of the Welcome Center Lot

The first 200 feet are the most awkward part of the whole ride. You're getting used to pedal feel that's slightly different from your car and a vehicle that's noticeably narrower than what you're used to judging. Then muscle memory takes over, fast.

## The First Time AutoDrive Shifts on Its Own

This is the single biggest confidence unlock for anyone who isn't already a motorcyclist. AutoDrive is an automated 5-speed — no clutch, nothing to learn — and the first time it downshifts on its own, most people relax noticeably. Don't fight it; let it do its job.

## Where the Road Opens Up — Onto the Trail

This is the payoff moment the whole first ten minutes builds toward. Open cockpit, real wind, the engine note right there with you, and sightlines over Ellis County farmland — bluebonnets in April, rolling green the rest of the season we're open.

## What Surprises First-Timers Most

The seating position, mostly — it's much lower than a car, so the road feels faster than the speedometer says. The steering is sharper than people expect too; reviewers have called it "razor-sharp," and that's accurate. And other drivers notice you. Expect waves.

## Is It Actually Scary?

No, not once you're past orientation. ABS, traction control, electronic stability control, and three-point belts are all standard equipment — the vehicle is engineered to be forgiving even though it feels raw.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are your experiences driving a Polaris Slingshot?

Consistently the same arc: nervous the first few minutes, comfortable by the first turn, grinning by the time the trail opens up.

### Is driving a Slingshot fun?

Yes — the open cockpit and direct steering make it feel like a different category of fun than driving a car, even at legal speeds.

### Is it scary to drive a Slingshot?

For the first few minutes, a little — then it clicks. The safety systems are all standard, and orientation exists specifically to get you past that initial nervousness.
