Lights, ice, cozy indoor spots, and one entry you can't actually do in December — here's the honest December date lineup.

## Holiday Light Displays Worth a Date

DFW runs several major light displays every winter, from park-wide walkthroughs to neighborhood drive-throughs. Hours and admission change year to year, so check each venue's current-season page before you plan around a specific date.

## Ice Skating and Winter Pop-Ups

Downtown and hotel-hosted ice rinks pop up seasonally across DFW — a classic, low-planning December date. Confirm current hours and pricing directly since these are seasonal builds that vary by year.

## Cozy Indoor Dates

A fireside bar, a holiday market, or dinner paired with a show all work well when the weather turns genuinely cold.

## Festive but Active Dates

A light-viewing walk or a downtown tree-lighting event both get you outside without requiring reservations months out.
