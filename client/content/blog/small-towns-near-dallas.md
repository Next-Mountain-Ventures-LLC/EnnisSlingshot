I'll say the honest thing up front: Ennis is my #1 pick on this list, but only in April. The rest of the year, Granbury or McKinney probably wins. Here's the full ranked list, organized by drive time from Dallas.

## Under 45 Minutes

**Ennis** — 35 miles, ~37 minutes on I-45. The Official Bluebonnet City of Texas (1997 Texas Legislature designation), home to 40+ miles of Ennis Garden Club trails. April only for the bluebonnets; Texas Motorplex and the National Polka Festival give it year-round reasons too, just not the same kind of visit.

**Waxahachie** — the 1897 courthouse and Scarborough Renaissance Festival make this a strong year-round pick, roughly 30–40 minutes from Dallas.

**Midlothian and Grapevine** round out the under-45-minute tier, each with their own distinct character worth a separate look.

## 45–75 Minutes

Granbury, Denton, Weatherford, and McKinney all sit in this range — each with enough of its own draw (historic squares, live music scenes, antique districts) to justify a dedicated day rather than a drive-by.

## 75+ Minutes

A few towns push past the hour-plus mark and are worth it if you're making a full day of the drive rather than a quick escape — confirm current drive times before committing to any of these for a tight schedule.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the coolest small town in Texas?

Depends what you're after — Ennis in April for wildflowers, Granbury or McKinney the rest of the year for classic small-town charm.

### What is the cutest town near Dallas?

Waxahachie's courthouse square and Granbury's historic downtown are both strong contenders, each with a different flavor.

### Where can I go for a day trip from Dallas?

See the ranked list above, organized by drive time so you can match the trip to how much of a day you have.
