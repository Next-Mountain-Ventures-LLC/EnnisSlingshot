No "starting at" games here. Here's exactly what's in every 2027 package and what it costs.

## What's Included in Every Package

| Included | Every Package |
| --- | --- |
| Fuel | Yes |
| Comprehensive insurance | Yes ($500 max out-of-pocket) |
| Orientation and safety briefing | Yes |
| Deposit | None |

## 1-Hour Drive & Go — $69.99

The no-dealer test drive — for anyone who wants to know what a Slingshot feels like without committing to a full 2-hour experience.

## 2-Hour Bluebonnet Trail Experience — Solo $79 / Two-Up $149

The flagship package — our full route through the Ennis Bluebonnet Trails. Solo is $79; bring a passenger for $149, and only the driver needs insurance approval. Read more about [why we call this an experience, not a rental](https://ennisslingshot.com/blog/what-is-a-slingshot-experience/).

## Date Night — Book the Driver + Rider Experience

There's no separate date-night package — couples book the 2-hour [Driver + Rider experience ($149)](https://ennisslingshot.com/book/?package=two-up), and I'd suggest a late-afternoon slot for golden-hour light if the timing works for your schedule.

## Groups — How a Group of Up to 4 Books With a 2-Vehicle Fleet

We run two Slingshots, so a group of 4 rides together — book both Slingshots for the same time slot at standard package pricing ($149 each with a rider), or email info@ennisslingshot.com and we'll set it up. There's no separate group package.

## Add-Ons: Bluetooth Communication Helmets ($25/helmet)

Lets driver and passenger actually talk over wind noise — genuinely useful on the two-person packages.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does it cost to rent a Slingshot for 2 hours?

$79 solo or $149 with a passenger.

### Is insurance included in a Slingshot rental?

Yes, on every package, with a $500 max out-of-pocket for vehicle damage or theft.

### Can two people ride in a Slingshot?

Yes, the Two-Up package is $149 and only the driver needs insurance approval.

### Can a group rent multiple Slingshots at once?

Yes, up to 4 riders across our two-vehicle fleet, each vehicle at standard package pricing.
