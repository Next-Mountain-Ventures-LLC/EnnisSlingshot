A Polaris Slingshot's 9.77-gallon tank, at an owner-reported 23–33 MPG, gets you well over 200 miles — more than enough for a 40-mile bluebonnet loop with plenty to spare.

## Polaris Slingshot Tank Size

9.77 gallons (37.1 liters), consistent across model years.

## What Fuel Does a Polaris Slingshot Take?

Per Polaris's own fuel recommendation page, the Slingshot needs unleaded gasoline with a 91 pump-octane minimum and no more than 10% ethanol (standard E10). Don't run E85 or anything with higher ethanol content — Polaris warns it can damage the engine.

## Polaris Slingshot MPG

Polaris hasn't published an official EPA-style MPG figure. Owner-reported mixed-driving mileage runs 23–33 MPG, averaging around 28.

## So How Far Can It Actually Go on a Full Tank?

At a 28 MPG average, 9.77 gallons works out to roughly 270 miles — more at the high end of owner-reported mileage, less at the low end.

| Tank Size | Owner-Reported MPG | Estimated Range |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 9.77 gal | 23–33 (avg ~28) | ~225–320 mi |
