Waxahachie's historic downtown runs a full December calendar — a living nativity, a candlelight homes tour, and courthouse-square lights — all within about 40 minutes of Dallas.

## Downtown Waxahachie in December

The courthouse square gets a real holiday atmosphere — lights, cold-weather foot traffic, a genuine small-town Christmas feel that's easy to miss if you only think of Waxahachie as a spring Scarborough Faire town.

## Bethlehem Revisited

Waxahachie's annual live-nativity walkthrough runs December 11–15, 2026, with evening hours (roughly 6–9pm, varying by night). It's a genuinely different kind of holiday event than a typical lights display — confirm the exact nightly schedule at bethlehem-revisited.com before you go.

## The Candlelight Tour of Homes

A dozen historic homes decorated for the season, toured over the first three weekends after Thanksgiving (Saturdays and Sundays, roughly 2–8pm) — that puts it in late November through early December 2026. Confirm current tickets and exact dates at the Waxahachie CVB before planning around it.

## Christmas Lights Worth the Drive

The courthouse square lighting is the anchor; check current neighborhood displays with the Waxahachie CVB for anything added this season.
