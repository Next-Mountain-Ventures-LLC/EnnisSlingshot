Two wheels up front, one driven wheel in back — that's not a styling choice. It's called a reverse trike, and the layout is the actual reason a Slingshot is legal to drive on a regular license.

## Reverse Trike vs. Delta Trike

Two common three-wheel layouts exist. A reverse trike — also called a tadpole layout — puts two wheels up front and one driven wheel in back; that's the Slingshot (and the Can-Am Ryker). A delta trike flips it: one wheel front, two in back, which is how most Harley trike conversions are built.

## Why 2 Front / 1 Rear Is More Stable in Corners

Two front contact patches give the vehicle more resistance to rolling in a turn than a single front wheel would — the same reason a tadpole-layout trike generally corners more predictably than a delta layout at speed.

## The Legal Payoff: How Wheel Count Creates the "Autocycle" Category

Tex. Transp. Code §501.008(a) specifically defines an autocycle as a vehicle with "not more than three wheels on the ground," plus seat belts, a steering mechanism, and non-straddle seating. The wheel count isn't incidental to the statute — it's a defining criterion. Meet it, along with the other three, and you get the autocycle classification that lets you drive one on a standard Class C license.

## What's Standard on the Slingshot's Chassis

ABS, traction control, and electronic stability control all come standard, working together to manage the specific dynamics of a reverse-trike layout. Curb weight runs around 1,651 lb.

## What This Means From the Driver's Seat

Every orientation I run includes a specific note on throttle discipline going into turns — the layout handles differently than a four-wheel car mid-corner, and it's worth knowing that before your first turn on the trail, not during it.

## What the Layout Doesn't Do

Three wheels and good stability systems don't add up to a car's full protection. No roll cage — there's a rear roll hoop, which is a different thing entirely — and no airbags. Honest limits, not a design flaw.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why does a Slingshot only have 3 wheels?

It's a specific engineering layout (reverse trike) that also happens to satisfy the legal definition of an autocycle in Texas and 48 other states.

### Is a 3-wheel car safe?

Reverse-trike layouts like the Slingshot's are generally considered more stable in corners than delta-layout trikes — see our full [safety features breakdown](https://ennisslingshot.com/blog/polaris-slingshot-safety-features/) for the complete equipment picture.

### What is a reverse trike?
