Fall is the best weather Dallas gets all year, and most "date ideas" lists don't bother dating themselves — they're the same 20 entries recycled every season with a new headline. This one isn't. Every pick below is tied to an actual fall window, not something you could just as easily do in June.

## State Fair of Texas Dates

The 2026 State Fair of Texas runs Friday, September 25 through Sunday, October 18 — 24 days, per Big Tex's own FAQ page. If crowds aren't your thing as a couple, go on a weekday afternoon rather than a weekend night; Fair Park's architecture alone is worth walking at dusk before you fight the Midway lines.

## Pumpkin Patches and Fall Displays

The Dallas Arboretum runs its Autumn at the Arboretum pumpkin display every fall — confirm current dates at dallasarboretum.org before you go, since they shift year to year. It's a genuinely photogenic, lower-key alternative to the Fair if you'd rather not deal with Midway crowds.

## A Race-Weekend Date at Texas Motorplex

I know this isn't most couples' first instinct for a date, but hear me out: the NHRA Texas Fall Nationals runs October 14–18, 2026 at Texas Motorplex, right here in Ennis. If your partner has even a passing interest in cars, a qualifying-day date (quieter, cheaper, easier to actually talk during) beats fighting Sunday's elimination crowds. I've written up the full [first-timer's weekend guide](https://ennisslingshot.com/blog/nhra-fall-nationals-texas-motorplex-guide/) and the [parking and gate-time logistics](https://ennisslingshot.com/blog/texas-motorplex-parking-and-traffic/) separately.

## Cozy Indoor Fall Dates

When the forecast doesn't cooperate: a wine bar, a cooking class, or an afternoon matinee all work as well in October as they do year-round — the fall angle here is just that they pair naturally with cooler evenings.

## Scenic Fall Drives

Cooler weather makes open-cockpit driving genuinely comfortable again after a Texas summer — which is exactly why I'll say plainly that our Slingshot rides through the Ennis Bluebonnet Trails don't run in the fall. We're April-only, tied to bloom season. If a scenic open-air drive sounds like your kind of date, put it on your spring calendar now rather than looking for it this October.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What can couples do in Dallas in the fall?

The State Fair, the Arboretum's pumpkin display, a Texas Motorplex race weekend, or a cozy indoor date if the weather turns — see the picks above.

### Is the State Fair of Texas a good date?

Yes, if you go at the right time — a weekday afternoon beats a Saturday night if you actually want to talk to each other instead of shuffling through a crowd.
