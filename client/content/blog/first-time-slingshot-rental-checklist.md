Bring your ID, sunglasses, sunscreen, a small bag, and a bit of cash for the Welcome Center. Leave the backpack and the big camera bag at home.

## Before You Arrive: Documents and Booking

After booking, you'll get a verification email — make sure the name on your ID matches your booking exactly. Arrive 15 minutes early, and double-check your appointment type (Solo, Two-Up, or Drive & Go) matches what you booked. See our [full approval process](https://ennisslingshot.com/slingshot-rental/requirements/) for details.

## What to Wear

Closed-toe shoes, sunglasses, layers for April's 78°F average high, and sunscreen. See our [full wardrobe guide](https://ennisslingshot.com/blog/what-to-wear-to-drive-a-slingshot/) for more.

## What to Bring

- Valid driver's license matching your approval email
- Phone (with a mount if you want photos or navigation)
- Sunglasses
- Sunscreen
- A small amount of cash for a Welcome Center stop
- A printed or digital copy of your booking confirmation

## What to Leave at Home (or in the Car)

Backpacks and large bags don't fit the storage compartments well — bring a small bag, wallet, or purse instead. Leave large camera bags, coolers, and loose items that could blow around in an open cockpit.

## Where Your Stuff Actually Goes

The Slingshot's gasketed storage compartment holds a small bag or purse comfortably — not a backpack or duffel. Keep loose items zipped in a bag rather than in your lap or an open cup holder, given the wind exposure of an open cockpit.

## The Night Before: A Quick Prep List

Charge your phone. Check the forecast — see our [weather and reschedule policy](https://ennisslingshot.com/blog/weather-reschedule-policy/) in case of rain. Review the meeting point address and parking. Set a reminder for the 15-minutes-early arrival.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What do I need to rent a Slingshot?

A valid driver's license matching your approval email, plus your booking confirmation. Drivers must be 21+.

### What should I bring to a Slingshot rental?

See the checklist above — ID, phone, sunglasses, sunscreen, cash, and your confirmation.

### Where do you put your stuff in a Slingshot?

A gasketed storage compartment — enough for a small bag, not a backpack.
