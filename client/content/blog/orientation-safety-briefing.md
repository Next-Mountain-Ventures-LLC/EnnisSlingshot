Nobody gets handed keys cold. Every booking includes about 15 minutes of orientation before the actual ride starts, and here's roughly how it breaks down.

## Check-In at the Welcome Center

Everything starts at the Ennis Welcome Center, 201 NW Main St. We confirm your booking and get you squared away before you even see the vehicle.

## The Walkaround

Before you get in, I walk you around the Slingshot itself — where the roll hoop sits, how the mirrors are positioned, the seat belts, and where the trail map lives in the glove box.

## Controls Explained

Steering wheel, pedals, AutoDrive basics — if you can drive a car, you can drive this. The honest truth is there's not much to explain mechanically; the bigger adjustment is the seating position and the wind, which no amount of talking fixes. That's what the next step is for.

## The Supervised Test Loop

A short, low-speed loop with staff present — this is the step that actually converts nerves into confidence, not the talking beforehand. Almost everyone finishes this loop more relaxed than they started it.

## The Helmet Conversation

Texas doesn't require a helmet for most riders 21 and older who've completed a training course or carry qualifying health insurance (Tex. Transp. Code §661.003), and it does require one under 21. Either way, we offer one to everyone at check-in — that's our call, not a legal minimum. Full detail in our [Texas Slingshot laws guide](https://ennisslingshot.com/blog/texas-slingshot-laws/).

## Hand-Back and the Ride Itself

Once orientation wraps, you're on your route. Staff stay reachable for the length of your booking — if something feels off once you're out there, that's what we're there for.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is a Polaris Slingshot hard to drive?

No, if you can drive a car — see our [common first-timer mistakes](https://ennisslingshot.com/blog/slingshot-driving-mistakes/) post for the handful of habits that trip people up.

### Do you need experience to drive a Slingshot?

No — that's exactly what orientation and the supervised test loop are for.

### Is a Slingshot safe?

See our full [safety equipment breakdown](https://ennisslingshot.com/blog/polaris-slingshot-safety-features/) for the complete answer.

### Do you have to wear a helmet in a Slingshot in Texas?

Only under 21, or 21+ without a training course or qualifying insurance — see our [full helmet guide](https://ennisslingshot.com/blog/do-i-need-a-helmet-for-a-polaris-slingshot/).
