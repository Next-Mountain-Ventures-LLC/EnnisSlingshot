**No — Texas law does not require a helmet for most adult Polaris Slingshot drivers.** But we hand you one anyway. Here's the exact rule, why the exemption isn't as simple as "if you're 21 you're fine," and what we actually do at Ennis Slingshot Experience. (For the full statute set — license, age, insurance too — see our [Texas Slingshot laws guide](https://ennisslingshot.com/blog/texas-slingshot-laws/).)

## What Texas Law Actually Says

Under Tex. Transp. Code §661.0015(b), an autocycle is treated as a motorcycle for helmet-law purposes — unless it has a partially or fully enclosed cab. A stock Polaris Slingshot has no cab at all, so the motorcycle helmet chapter applies to it in full.

## The Under-21 Rule

Straightforward: if you're under 21, you must wear a helmet. No exemption applies.

## The 21-and-Over Exemption — and Why It's Not Automatic

Riders 21 and older are exempt from the helmet requirement under Tex. Transp. Code §661.003 — but only if they've completed a motorcycle operator training/safety course **or** carry a health insurance plan that covers injury from a collision. Simply being 21 doesn't clear you. The fine for noncompliance runs $10–$50, and police may not stop or detain a rider solely to check for helmet-law compliance.

## What We Do at Ennis Slingshot

Regardless of who qualifies for the legal exemption, we provide a helmet for every driver and every passenger on every ride. Open cockpit, real speed, wind, bugs, sun, and unfamiliar roads add up — it's simply a better ride with one on, exemption or not.

## What Kind of Helmet Do You Actually Wear in a Slingshot?

Most renters do best in an open-face or half-helmet rather than a full-face motorcycle helmet — visibility and comfort matter more here than on a motorcycle, since you're not leaning into the wind the same way. Eye protection matters just as much as the helmet itself: there's no windshield, so sunglasses or goggles aren't optional extras, they're part of the kit.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do you have to wear a helmet in a Slingshot in Texas?

Only if you're under 21, or 21+ without a training course or qualifying health insurance. Either way, we provide one for every rider.

### What kind of helmet do you wear in a Slingshot?

Most riders prefer an open-face or half-helmet for visibility and comfort, paired with eye protection.

### Can I bring my own helmet?

Yes — we provide one, but you're welcome to bring your own if you prefer.
