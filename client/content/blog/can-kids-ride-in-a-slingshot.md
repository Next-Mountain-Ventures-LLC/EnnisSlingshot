Parents ask me this one constantly, and it deserves a careful answer rather than a quick yes or no — this is a child-safety question.

## What Texas Law Actually Says About Minor Passengers

Tex. Transp. Code §521.085(b) covers the driver's license requirement and §501.008 defines the autocycle as equipped with seat belts — neither directly addresses a minimum passenger age. Whether a separate statute (such as §545.416, which governs child passenger safety generally) specifically applies to autocycle passengers isn't clearly spelled out — it's a genuine gray area, not a settled rule.

## What Polaris Says About Seating

I haven't found a specific published passenger-age guideline from Polaris itself for the Slingshot. What is documented: side-by-side bucket seats with three-point belts, sized for adult occupants.

## Why There's No Confirmed Car Seat Option

I'm not aware of a car-seat anchor system in the Slingshot's seats — check Polaris's owner's manual if you need a definitive answer for a specific model. What I can say confidently: this vehicle was not designed with child car seats as a use case the way a minivan was.
