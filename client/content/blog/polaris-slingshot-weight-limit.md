I'd rather give you a straight, respectful answer than a vague "call us" non-response. Here's what I actually know.

## How Many Seats Does a Polaris Slingshot Have?

Two — side-by-side bucket seats, driver and one passenger, both with three-point belts.

## What Is the Weight Limit on a Polaris Slingshot?

Polaris doesn't publish one universal passenger weight figure. What I can tell you: roughly 275 lb per seat is the norm several Texas rental operators (San Antonio, Fort Worth listings) publish. That's the industry norm — if you're near that figure and want a definitive answer for our fleet specifically, ask us directly.

## Why Rental Operators Set a Per-Seat Guideline

It's not an arbitrary cutoff — it's combined payload across two seats plus fuel, at a curb weight of roughly 1,651 lb. The math has to work for the vehicle to handle and perform the way it's designed to.

## Seat Width, Belt Fit and Comfort

Bucket seats with adjustable three-point belts — I cover the full comfort picture, including entry technique and what a 2-hour ride actually feels like, in a [separate post](https://ennisslingshot.com/blog/is-a-polaris-slingshot-comfortable/).

## Is There a Weight Limit to Rent a Slingshot?

Most operators, including us, work within some combined per-seat guideline. There's no judgment in asking ahead of time — it's a straightforward logistics question, and we'd rather answer it honestly before you book than have it be a surprise at check-in.
