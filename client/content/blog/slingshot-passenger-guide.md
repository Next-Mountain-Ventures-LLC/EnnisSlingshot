Half the questions I field before a two-person booking aren't from the driver — they're about the passenger seat. Here's the mechanics, no fluff.

## Do You Need Anything Special to Ride as a Passenger?

No. Only the driver needs the insurance-approval link and has to be 21+. The passenger seat has zero paperwork.

|  | Driver | Passenger |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Age | 21+ | No requirement |
| License | Standard Class C | None needed |
| Insurance approval | Required after booking | None needed |

## What the Passenger Seat Actually Feels Like

Genuinely different from driving — no pedals or wheel to manage means more time to actually take in the view. Side-by-side seating puts you right next to the driver, not behind.

## Weight & Fit Guidance for the Right Seat

Most Slingshot rental operators use roughly 275 lb per seat as a guideline. If you or your rider are close to that, email us at info@ennisslingshot.com before you book and we'll make sure you're a comfortable fit.

## What to Hold Onto

There's a grab handle on the passenger side specifically for this — use it through turns rather than bracing against the door frame (there isn't one).

## Talking to Your Driver at Speed — Bluetooth Helmets Explained

Wind noise makes normal conversation nearly impossible once you're moving. For $25 per helmet, our Bluetooth communication add-on solves that — driver and passenger can actually talk through the whole ride instead of shouting.

## Shooting Video Safely as a Passenger

A phone mount beats handheld filming — don't reach or lean while the vehicle's moving. The passenger seat is genuinely a great vantage point for content; just don't let filming become a distraction for either of you.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can two people ride in a Polaris Slingshot?

Yes — side-by-side bucket seats, both with three-point belts.

### Does the passenger need a license in a Slingshot?

No — only the driver needs a standard Class C license.

### How do you talk to your passenger in a Slingshot?

Our Bluetooth communication helmet add-on ($25/helmet) is the practical answer — shouting over wind noise gets old fast.
