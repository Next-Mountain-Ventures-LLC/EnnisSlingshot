Is it difficult to drive a Slingshot? Not mechanically — if you can drive a car, you can drive one. But a handful of car habits actively work against you at first, and these are the seven I watch for on the supervised test loop before every ride.

## Mistake 1 — Fighting the AutoDrive Transmission

**Why it happens:** people expect the smooth, seamless feel of a CVT. AutoDrive downshifts on its own when you lift off the throttle, and it feels like engine braking. **The fix:** let it shift. Don't ride the brake to compensate for something that isn't a problem.

## Mistake 2 — Forgetting the Rear Wheel Tracks Inside the Front Wheels on Tight Turns

**Why it happens:** car intuition doesn't map onto reverse-trike geometry. **The fix:** give tight corners more room than instinct tells you to.

## Mistake 3 — Braking Like It's a Car

**Why it happens:** braking feel is closer to a motorcycle's than a car's, per rental-reviewer testimony. **The fix:** leave more following distance, brake earlier than you would in your daily driver.

## Mistake 4 — Skipping Sunscreen and Sunglasses

**Why it happens:** open cockpit, no windshield tint, no shade. **The fix:** apply before you arrive, and wear actual sunglasses — squinting for two hours isn't a strategy.

## Mistake 5 — Over-Driving the First Mile

**Why it happens:** adrenaline, plus a supervised test loop that felt easier than the open road. **The fix:** ease into speed over the first few minutes of the actual route instead of matching your test-loop confidence immediately.

## Mistake 6 — Not Adjusting Mirrors Before Moving

**Why it happens:** people rush past the walkaround step because they want to drive. **The fix:** the extra 30 seconds is worth it.

## Mistake 7 — Treating the Passenger Seat as an Afterthought

**Why it happens:** drivers focus on themselves and forget to brief whoever's riding along. **The fix:** a quick passenger briefing — hold on through turns, expect sun exposure — takes ten seconds and saves a surprised passenger.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is it difficult to drive a Slingshot?

Not mechanically — but a few car habits work against you at first. The seven mistakes above cover them.

### How to correctly use a Slingshot?

Steering wheel, pedals, AutoDrive — see our full [orientation walkthrough](https://ennisslingshot.com/blog/orientation-safety-briefing/) for how we cover it in person.

### What should you not do in a Slingshot?

Don't fight the transmission, don't brake like it's a car, and don't skip the mirror adjustment — the top three from the list above.
