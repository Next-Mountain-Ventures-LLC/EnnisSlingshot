Learn about the beautiful bluebonnet trails around Ennis and the best times to visit.

Every spring, the fields around Ennis erupt in a sea of blue as the state flower of Texas comes into full bloom. The Ennis Bluebonnet Trails wind through more than 40 miles of official routes, making it one of the best places in the state to see them up close.

## Best Time to Visit

Peak bloom typically runs from early April through late April, though it can shift a week or two depending on rainfall. Weekday mornings tend to be quieter if you want the trails mostly to yourself.

Pair your bluebonnet trip with a Slingshot rental for an open-air view of the fields that you simply can't get from behind a windshield.
