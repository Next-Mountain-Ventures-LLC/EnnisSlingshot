Anniversary gift themes give you a real structure to plan around instead of another generic "romantic date" list — here's a Dallas-area date matched to where you actually are in the relationship.

## Year 1 (Paper) — Low-Key and Sentimental

A quiet dinner somewhere you can actually hear each other, or a handwritten-letter exchange over coffee — Year 1 doesn't need a production.

## Years 2–5 (Cotton, Wood, Fruit/Flowers) — Active and Adventurous

This is where a couple's activity level usually peaks — a hike, a new restaurant neighborhood, something with a little movement built in.

## Years 10–15 (Tin, Crystal) — A Splurge

A nicer dinner than usual, or a weekend trip if the calendar allows — these milestones tend to justify spending a bit more than a typical date night.

## Years 20–25 (China, Silver) — Milestone

A real milestone deserves a real occasion — this is where I'd point people toward something they'll actually remember specifically, not just "a nice dinner we had."

## Recreate Your First Date

Go back to the same neighborhood, order what you ordered the first time, walk the same route home if you can. It costs nothing extra and it lands every time.
