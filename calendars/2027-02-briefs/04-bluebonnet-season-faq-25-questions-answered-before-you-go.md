## Facts & sources to cite
- Trails run April 1–30 every year; driving them is free — bluebonnettrail.org (business-context.md).
- Festival gate pricing (2026 season): $5 adults, 12 and under free, cashless/gate-only tickets — visitennistexas.com; Incredible Texas; festivalnet (audience-market-research.md §4a). Note this is 2026 pricing cited as the reference point — do not present as confirmed 2027 pricing without a "check current pricing" caveat.
- Peak bloom is typically the third week of April, varies by weather and terrain — visitennistexas.com; bluebonnettrail.org/trailmap (audience-market-research.md §4a).
- Attendance is disputed: Focus Daily News (2021, quoting the city) says 15,000–20,000 for festival weekend; festivalnet lists 35,000 (promoter-supplied) — cite both, never the unsourced 100,000+ figure (audience-market-research.md §4b).
- Ennis Welcome Center, 201 NW Main St, (972) 878-4748, April hours Mon–Fri 8–6, Sat 9–5, Sun 11–4 — bluebonnettrail.org; ennistx.gov (audience-market-research.md §4a).
- Ennis is 35 miles / ~37 minutes south of downtown Dallas on I-45 — Travelmath (audience-market-research.md §4f).
- Is it illegal to pick bluebonnets in Texas — no blanket state law bans it, but stay off posted private property; full analysis in the dedicated earlier post (bb-035) rather than restated here.
- No source confirms a pet policy at the trails or festival — mark "verify with the Welcome Center" if addressed.
