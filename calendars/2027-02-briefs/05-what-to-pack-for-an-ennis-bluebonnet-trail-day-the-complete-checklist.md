## Facts & sources to cite
- Ennis April climate: average high 78°F, low 55°F, ~26% chance of rain on a given day — Wanderlog/WeatherSpark (audience-market-research.md §2d).
- Recurring visitor tips: rural roads have potholes, stay on roads and off private property, watch for fire ants and snakes — TripAdvisor-sourced (audience-market-research.md §4c).
- Fire ant and snake specifics are covered in the dedicated earlier posts (bb-020, bb-019) — link rather than re-explaining the biology/first-aid detail here.
- The Slingshot has an open cockpit — no roof, no HVAC, water-resistant interior and sealed/gasketed storage; expect to get wet in rain — Snow Goer; Car Talk; Slingshot Forums (audience-market-research.md §2d).
- Helmets are not legally required for autocycles like the Slingshot in Texas for riders 21+ with a qualifying exemption (safety course or health insurance), but riders under 21 must wear one — Tex. Transp. Code §661.0015(b), §661.003 (audience-market-research.md §2a). Most operators, including Ennis Slingshot, provide/recommend helmets regardless.
- Ennis Welcome Center, 201 NW Main St, (972) 878-4748 — bluebonnettrail.org; ennistx.gov (audience-market-research.md §4a) — mention as the first stop to grab a paper trail map.
- Bluetooth communication helmet add-on available for $25/helmet — business-context.md (mention only if the post covers the Slingshot-specific packing section).
