## Differentiation (information gain)
1. **A real step-by-step plan, not a spot list.** Existing bluebonnet content (photographer blogs, DFWChild, WFAA's "8 places") is either a numbered location list or a photographer's portfolio page — none of it sequences an actual date from "leave Dallas" to "dinner in downtown Ennis." This post is the missing itinerary: pick the week using the bloom tracker → leave Dallas by 3 pm → which loop to drive → where to stop for photos without trampling private land → dinner downtown.
2. **The picking-flowers myth, addressed head-on and correctly.** Many couples still believe picking bluebonnets is a felony in Texas — it isn't, but stopping on private property or highway right-of-way without permission is trespassing/a traffic hazard. A direct, statute-aware answer (with a link to the site's dedicated legality post once published) builds trust competitors' photo-only content skips entirely.
3. **Two ways to do it — self-drive or a booked Slingshot route — both covered honestly.** Every other bluebonnet date guide assumes the reader drives their own car. This post gives the plan for both, including what changes if you book the 2-hour Bluebonnet Trail Experience (guided route, no parking-on-the-shoulder guesswork, insurance included).
