## FAQ (PAA-driven)
- Does a Polaris Slingshot have airbags? — No; open-cockpit, motorcycle-classified vehicle, no airbags on any trim.
- Does a Slingshot have a roll cage? — No, a rear roll hoop, not a cage; explain the difference in one sentence.
- Does a Polaris Slingshot have seat belts? — Yes, three-point belts on both seats, same mechanism as a car's.
