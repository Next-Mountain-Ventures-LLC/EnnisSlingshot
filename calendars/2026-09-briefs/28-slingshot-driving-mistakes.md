## Structure
Answer-first intro (~120w, TL;DR: is it hard to drive — no, but these 7 habits trip people up) · then, each entry as "Mistake → Why it happens → The fix":
1. **H2: Mistake 1 — Fighting the AutoDrive Transmission** — Why: expecting a CVT's smoothness; it downshifts like engine braking. Fix: let it shift, don't ride the brake to compensate.
2. **H2: Mistake 2 — Forgetting the Rear Wheel Tracks Inside the Front Wheels on Tight Turns** — Why: car intuition doesn't apply to the reverse-trike geometry. Fix: give tight corners more room than you think you need.
3. **H2: Mistake 3 — Braking Like It's a Car** — Why: braking feel is closer to a motorcycle's (per rental-reviewer testimony). Fix: leave more following distance, brake earlier.
4. **H2: Mistake 4 — Skipping Sunscreen and Sunglasses** — Why: open cockpit, no windshield tint. Fix: apply before you arrive, wear real sunglasses not just squinting.
5. **H2: Mistake 5 — Over-Driving the First Mile** — Why: adrenaline plus a supervised test loop that felt easy. Fix: ease into speed over the first few minutes of the actual route.
6. **H2: Mistake 6 — Not Adjusting Mirrors Before Moving** — Why: rushing past the walkaround step. Fix: take the extra 30 seconds.
7. **H2: Mistake 7 — Treating the Passenger Seat as an Afterthought** — Why: drivers forget to brief their passenger. Fix: quick passenger briefing on holding on through turns, sun exposure.
