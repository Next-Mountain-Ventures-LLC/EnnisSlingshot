## Facts & sources to cite
- Ennis Bluebonnet Trails: "over 40 miles of mapped driving Bluebonnet Trails," sponsored by the Ennis Garden Club, "the oldest such trails known in the State of Texas" — bluebonnettrail.org: https://www.bluebonnettrail.org/
- 1952 first trails drew approximately 1,000 visitors; grew to 10,000 within three years — HMdb historical marker: https://www.hmdb.org/m.asp?m=269579
- Trails open April 1–30 every year — Ellis County: https://www.elliscountytx.gov/323/Bluebonnet-Trail
- Three official routes (north, south, west) plus public photo locations — bluebonnettrail.org / Local Profile: https://www.localprofile.com/things-to-do/ennis-bluebonnet-trails-road-trip-guide-7504621
- 1997 Texas Legislature designation as home of the Official Texas Bluebonnet Trail and Official Bluebonnet City of Texas — Ellis County: https://www.elliscountytx.gov/323/Bluebonnet-Trail
- Ennis Welcome Center, 201 NW Main St, Ennis, TX 75119, source of trail maps — City of Ennis: https://www.ennistx.gov/Attractions
- Free "Ennis Y'all" app with GPS trail map — City of Ennis: https://www.ennistx.gov/ennis-yall-mobile-app
- Washington Post covered Ennis, "Bluebonnets are king in the Texas town of Ennis" (Apr 4, 2025) — headline-only, paywalled: https://www.washingtonpost.com/travel/2025/04/04/ennis-texas-bluebonnet-festival/
