## Facts & sources to cite
- Bluebonnets typically peak "around the third week of April" in North Texas — visitennistexas.com / bluebonnettrail.org
- Ennis Bluebonnet Trails run April 1–30 every year, ~40 miles of mapped trails, sponsored by the Ennis Garden Club — bluebonnettrail.org
- Google's AI Overview for "bluebonnet season texas" explicitly states "North Texas (Dallas, Ennis): Flowers peak during April," citing mickeyshannon.com, wildflower.org and others — `serp-intelligence.md` §1/§3
- Statewide bloom experts predicted a "moderate" 2026 season after a dry fall and warm winter — FOX 7 Austin, https://www.fox7austin.com/news/texas-experts-predict-moderate-bluebonnet-season-due-dry-fall-warm-winter
- Bluebonnets are the Texas state flower (*Lupinus texensis*); germination and bloom timing depend on fall/winter rainfall — Lady Bird Johnson Wildflower Center, wildflower.org
- Ennis named "Official Bluebonnet City of Texas" by the 1997 Texas Legislature — elliscountytx.gov
- Ennis April climate: average high 78°F, low 55°F, ~26% chance of rain on a given day — Wanderlog/WeatherSpark (`audience-market-research.md` §2d)
- Ennis Welcome Center, 201 NW Main St, Ennis, TX 75119, (972) 878-4748 — ennistx.gov / bluebonnettrail.org
