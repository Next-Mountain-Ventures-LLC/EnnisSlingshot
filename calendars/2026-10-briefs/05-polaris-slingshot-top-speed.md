1. **Trim-specific numbers, not one flat figure.** Most SERP results and forum posts quote a single "top speed" number without saying which of the six 2026 trims it applies to. 2. **Distinguishes top speed from 0–60 explicitly**, correcting a common conflation in search results (per the secondary keyword `polaris slingshot 0-60 vs top speed`) — the R trim's sourced 0–60 of ~4.9 seconds is a straight-line acceleration figure, not a top-speed claim, and the post says so.
