## Facts & sources to cite
- Ennis is 35 miles / ~37 minutes south of downtown Dallas on I-45 — Travelmath: https://www.travelmath.com/drive-distance/from/Ennis,+TX/to/Dallas,+TX
- Ennis Bluebonnet Trails open April 1–30, 40+ miles of mapped routes, sponsored by the Ennis Garden Club — bluebonnettrail.org: https://www.bluebonnettrail.org/
- Ennis was designated Texas's Official Bluebonnet City by the 1997 Texas Legislature — Ellis County: https://www.elliscountytx.gov/323/Bluebonnet-Trail
- Ennis Welcome Center, 201 NW Main St, Ennis TX 75119, (972) 878-4748, April hours Mon–Fri 8–6, Sat 9–5, Sun 11–4 — bluebonnettrail.org / City of Ennis: https://www.ennistx.gov/Attractions
- Ennis Slingshot's 1-Hour Drive & Go is $69.99, insurance included, no deposit — internal, `seo/research/business-context.md`
- No motorcycle license required to drive a Slingshot in Texas — Tex. Transp. Code §521.085(b): https://texas.public.law/statutes/tex._transp._code_section_521.085
