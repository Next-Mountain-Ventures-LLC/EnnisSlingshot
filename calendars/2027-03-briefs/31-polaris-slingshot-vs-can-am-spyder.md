> **Get the legal distinction right — this is the whole point of the post.** The Can-Am Spyder is legally a **motorcycle** in Texas: straddle seating, handlebar steering, and a motorcycle license (Class M) or endorsement is generally required. The Polaris Slingshot is legally an **autocycle**: side-by-side seating, steering wheel and pedals, drivable on a standard Class C license (Tex. Transp. Code §521.085(b); §501.008 defines "autocycle"). Do not blur these — a reader who confuses the two could show up expecting to drive a Spyder on a car license and be turned away.
