## Facts & sources to cite
- Katy Trail: 3.5-mile paved urban trail, leash required, doggy water/waste stations along the route — Katy Trail Dallas, https://katytraildallas.org/trail-info/ and https://katytraildallas.org/2022/05/safety-trail-tips/
- White Rock Lake (Mockingbird Point) Dog Park: 3-acre off-leash park, separate large/small dog areas, Tue–Sun 5am–11pm, closed Mondays — Dallas Parks & Recreation, https://www.dallasparks.org/Facilities/Facility/Details/Mockingbird-Point-Dog-Park-358
- Bishop Arts dog-friendly patio culture (Cane Rosso, Dallas Grilled Cheese Co., Lockhart Smokehouse, Spiral Diner) — D Magazine, "The Best Dog-Friendly Restaurants and Bars in North Texas," https://www.dmagazine.com/guides/the-best-dog-friendly-restaurants-and-bars-in-north-texas/
- Waxahachie dog-friendly dining (Vita Coffee House, Railport Brewing Company, Fuzzy's Taco Shop) — BringFido, https://www.bringfido.com/restaurant/city/waxahachie_tx_us/
- Bardwell Lake pets must be leashed (under 6 ft) or otherwise restrained per U.S. Army Corps of Engineers Fort Worth District regulations — USACE Bardwell Lake, https://www.swf-wc.usace.army.mil/bardwell/Recreation/Parks/Corpsparks.shtml
- Ennis Welcome Center, 201 NW Main St, Ennis, TX 75119 — City of Ennis Attractions, https://www.ennistx.gov/Attractions
- Ennis is 35 miles / ~37 minutes from downtown Dallas on I-45 — cited in `seo/research/audience-market-research.md` §4f
- No motorcycle license required to drive a Polaris Slingshot in Texas; standard Class C license is sufficient — Tex. Transp. Code §521.085(b), https://texas.public.law/statutes/tex._transp._code_section_521.085
- Slingshot seating: 2 side-by-side human seats, insurance covers driver + one approved passenger only — `seo/research/business-context.md`
