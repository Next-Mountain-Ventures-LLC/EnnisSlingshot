## FAQ (PAA-driven)
- Can you drive a Slingshot at night? — yes, legally and mechanically; restate the golden-hour recommendation instead of encouraging true after-dark driving.
- Does a Polaris Slingshot have good headlights? — standard LED lighting, adequate and DOT-compliant, but the vehicle's low ride height matters more for how visible *you* are to others than how much *you* can see.
- Is it safe to drive a Slingshot in the dark? — same standard safety systems as daytime (ABS, stability control, three-point belts) apply after dark; the added variables are your own visibility to other traffic and temperature drop, not the vehicle itself.
