## FAQ (PAA-driven)
- What is Ennis, Texas famous for? — the Official Texas Bluebonnet Trail and Bluebonnet City of Texas designation (1997), plus Texas Motorplex.
- Is the Ennis Bluebonnet Trail free? — yes, driving the public-road routes is free; the wildflowers themselves are seasonal (April), and any guided/paid experience (like a Slingshot ride) is optional, not required.
