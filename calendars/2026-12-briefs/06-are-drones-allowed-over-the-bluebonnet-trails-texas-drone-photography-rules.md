## Facts & sources to cite
- Drones over 0.55 lbs must be registered with the FAA — Federal Aviation Administration, UAS registration: https://www.faa.gov/uas
- Recreational flyers must follow the rules for recreational flyers under 49 U.S.C. §44809 — FAA: https://www.faa.gov/uas/recreational_fliers
- FAA's B4UFLY app helps operators check airspace restrictions before flying — FAA: https://www.faa.gov/uas/getting_started/b4ufly
- Texas Government Code Chapter 423 restricts capturing images with a drone with intent to conduct surveillance of private property or individuals without consent — Texas Government Code: https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/GV/htm/GV.423.htm
- Ennis Bluebonnet Trails run April 1–30 across 40+ miles of mapped routes on a mix of public roads and private ranchland — bluebonnettrail.org: https://www.bluebonnettrail.org/
- Meadow View Nature Area and Bluebonnet Park are public land within the trail system — City of Ennis, Attractions: https://www.ennistx.gov/Attractions
- Criminal trespass and private-land access along the trails, covered in depth — Tex. Penal Code §30.05: https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/PE/htm/PE.30.htm
