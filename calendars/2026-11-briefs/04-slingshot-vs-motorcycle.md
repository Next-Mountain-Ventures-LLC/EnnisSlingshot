## Deliberate omissions / guardrails
- No motorcycle license required for a Slingshot in Texas — a standard Class C license is sufficient because the Slingshot is legally an autocycle (Tex. Transp. Code §521.085(b), §501.008). A motorcycle requires a Class M license.
- Helmet law: autocycles follow motorcycle helmet rules under §661.0015; riders 21+ are exempt under §661.003 if they've completed a safety course or have qualifying health insurance; under-21 riders must wear a helmet. Ennis Slingshot requires helmets for all renters regardless of the exemption (operator policy, not a legal requirement for 21+).
- Vehicle: ProStar 2.0L 4-cylinder engine, 180 hp (S/SL) or 204 hp (SLR/R/Signature/Grand Touring) by trim — never "1.5L twin-cylinder, 203 HP."
- Standard safety equipment: ABS, traction control, electronic stability control, three-point seat belts; no airbags, no roll cage (rear roll hoop only).
- No invented crash-statistics comparisons between Slingshots and motorcycles — no reliable head-to-head safety data exists; say so rather than fabricate a number.
- 21+ to drive at Ennis Slingshot; no 2027 festival dates as fact.
