## Differentiation (information gain)
1. States our actual, specific policy in plain language (operate rain or shine unless conditions are unsafe; weather or mechanical issues mean no refund but a free reschedule at no cost) rather than a vague "check the weather" disclaimer, which is what most rental FAQ pages offer.
2. Pairs the policy with real April climate normals for Ennis (78°F average high, 55°F average low, ~26% chance of rain on a given day) so readers can actually gauge their own odds, not just read a policy in the abstract.
3. Explains *what makes a ride "unsafe"* in concrete terms (high wind, lightning, standing water on the trail routes) rather than leaving "unsafe" undefined, which is a gap in every competitor FAQ reviewed in our research.

## Deliberate omissions / guardrails
- State the actual policy precisely: operates rain or shine unless conditions are unsafe; weather or mechanical issues → no refund, but a full reschedule at no cost; free rescheduling up to 7 days before the date otherwise, subject to availability — `business-context.md`.
- A Slingshot has no roof and no HVAC by default (Slingshade roof is an optional trim feature Ennis Slingshot does not currently offer) — do not imply our fleet has weather protection it doesn't have.
- Do not promise a specific refund policy beyond what's stated — no refunds for weather/mechanical cancellations, reschedule only.
- April climate normals are historical averages, not a forecast for any specific date — don't imply certainty about a future date's weather.
- No 2027 festival dates; no invented reviews.
