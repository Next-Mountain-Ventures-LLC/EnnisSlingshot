## Facts & sources to cite
- 92% of Americans would rather receive an experiential gift, up from 77% the prior year — GetYourGuide survey via Travel Agent Central, 2026: https://www.travelagentcentral.com/data-trends/stats-92-americans-would-rather-receive-experiential-gifts
- 82% prefer an experience gift over a physical one — Tinggly survey, same source.
- Three-quarters of respondents increased experiential spending, citing better perceived value than products; more than half say they "have too many things already" — Morning Consult, June 2025: https://pro.morningconsult.com/analysis/experience-economy-consumer-research
- 61% give experience gifts to create a "special memory"; 55% to give something "unique" — Square: https://squareup.com/us/en/the-bottom-line/reaching-customers/experiential-holiday-gifts
- "experience gifts dallas": 90 TX/mo average, 480/mo in December — DataForSEO, `keywords.md`, pulled 2026-09-04.
- "experience gifts for him": 1,900 US/mo average, December peak 8,100 (4.3x the average) — DataForSEO, pulled 2026-09-05.
- "experience gifts for her": 210 US/mo average, March and December both peak at 480 — DataForSEO, pulled 2026-09-05.
- Polaris says its average Slingshot customer age dropped to 48 in 2020, and over 80% of new Slingshot buyers were new to Polaris — Motorcycle.com, Dec 2020: https://www.motorcycle.com/manufacturer/polaris-manufacturer/2021-polaris-slingshot-first-ride-review.html *(cite as Slingshot-owner data, explicitly not experience-gift-buyer data — do not conflate)*
