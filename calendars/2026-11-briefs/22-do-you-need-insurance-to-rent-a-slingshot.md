## Deliberate omissions / guardrails
- A Slingshot is legally classified as an autocycle (a type of motorcycle) under Tex. Transp. Code §501.008, even though it drives like a car and needs only a standard Class C license under §521.085(b) — state both facts precisely, they are not contradictory but are commonly confused.
- Do not claim, as fact, that all personal auto policies exclude Slingshots — insurance policies vary by carrier and state; frame this as "check your policy's motorcycle/motorsport exclusions" rather than a blanket claim. Mark anything not directly sourced as "verify with your own insurer."
- Do not claim, as fact, that all credit cards exclude motorcycle-class rentals — most major card rental-car benefits do exclude motorcycles/off-road vehicles per their public terms, but cite this generally and recommend the reader check their specific card's benefits guide rather than naming a specific card issuer's terms as universal.
- State our own policy accurately: comprehensive insurance included with every rental, $500 max out-of-pocket for vehicle damage or theft, no deposit — `business-context.md`.
- No motorcycle license required to drive (§521.085(b)); driver 21+; season April only; no 2027 festival dates; no invented reviews.
