## Headline findings
1. **The whole niche is low-difficulty.** KD for "slingshot rental dallas" is **2**, "rent a slingshot" **7**, "polaris slingshot rental" **11**, "slingshot rental" **28**, "slingshot rental near me" **25**. Every bluebonnet and DFW-activity keyword we checked is KD 0–13. A new site with a real cluster can rank.
2. **Two demand curves, two seasons.** Bluebonnet demand is a March–April spike (Ennis Bluebonnet Festival: 14,800 TX searches in April, 6,600 in March, ~100 the rest of the year). Slingshot-rental demand is a summer curve (TX "slingshot rental" peaks 3,600 in March *and* June; "slingshot rental dallas" peaks 2,900 in **June**). The business is open only in April — we own April, and the content calendar should make the summer decision data-driven.
3. **DFW is the catchment, Houston is the bigger city term.** TX volumes: slingshot rental houston 1,300 · dallas 880 · galveston 390 · san antonio 320 · austin 50 · fort worth 40 · arlington 40 · waco 10. Suburb-level terms (Plano, Frisco, Waxahachie, etc.) return no measurable volume — city landing pages should be limited to Dallas / Fort Worth / Arlington (+ a Houston/Texas-wide page if the owner ever wants to draw statewide), *not* a 20-suburb spray.
4. **Adjacent "things to do / date ideas" demand dwarfs the category.** TX: date ideas dallas 2,400 · things to do in waxahachie 1,900 · weekend getaways from dallas 1,600 · couples activities dallas 1,600 · things to do near dallas 1,000 · day trips from dallas 880 · things to do in midlothian 480 · things to do in ennis tx 390 · birthday ideas dallas 320 · anniversary ideas dallas 210 · romantic getaways near dallas 140 · experience gifts dallas 90 (480 in Dec). The only DFW competitor's non-brand traffic comes almost entirely from *one* "top 10 things to do in Dallas" post.
5. **Massive adjacent events in our backyard.** Scarborough Renaissance Festival (Waxahachie, runs April–May): 27,100 avg / **135,000 in April** TX. Texas Motorplex (Ennis, NHRA): 12,100 avg / 40,500 in March, 33,100 in October. Both are same-weekend, same-drive audiences.
6. **Informational Slingshot questions are national and steady.** US: polaris slingshot top speed 1,300 · do you need a motorcycle license for a slingshot 590 (KD 5) · what is a polaris slingshot 170 · 3 wheel car rental 140 · polaris slingshot autodrive 110 · is a polaris slingshot a motorcycle 90 · how to drive a slingshot 40 · polaris slingshot in rain 40. "polaris slingshot" itself: 74,000 US, KD 20.
7. **Caution:** "slingshot ride" (1,900 TX / 22,200 US) is mostly the amusement-park reverse-bungee ride, not the vehicle. Don't target it.
