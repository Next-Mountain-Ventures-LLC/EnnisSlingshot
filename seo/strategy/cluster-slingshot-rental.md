```
                                      renting-vs-buying-a-polaris-slingshot

Hub-to-hub bridge: t-pillar ⇄ i-pillar (recommended, bidirectional)
```

### Template palette used (constrained to the 12 allowed types)
`pillar-page` (2), `faq-knowledge` (10), `product-review` (5), `how-to-guide` (2), `tutorial` (1), `listicle` (1), `data-research` (1), `comparison` (2).

Rationale for non-obvious mappings: the allowed template list is blog/content-oriented, not commerce-landing-page-oriented. Area pages and booking/trust pages were mapped to `faq-knowledge` because their SERPs (and PAA banks) are dominated by direct-answer, Q&A-structured content (hours/distance/what's-included/policy questions) rather than narrative guides. Package pages were mapped to `product-review` because each functions as an in-depth breakdown of one specific offering (what's included, who it's for, price) — the closest fit in the allowed palette to a commerce landing page.

## 8. Top 5 Opportunities

1. **`/blog/texas-slingshot-laws/`** — "do you need a motorcycle license for a slingshot in texas" (590 US, KD 5). Google's AI Overview currently cites a competitor (`slingshotrentalssa.com/faq`) alongside a source that *contradicts* the AIO's own answer (`fullthrottlehouston.com`). A clearly-sourced, TX Transportation Code–cited page is a direct, winnable AIO-citation target — nothing DFW-specific currently competes here.
2. **`/slingshot-rental/near-fort-worth/`** — "slingshot rental fort worth" (40 TX, KD 0). No independent competitor has a dedicated Fort Worth page; only Cloud of Goods' generic templated listing ranks. Lowest-difficulty, genuine gap in the whole keyword set.
3. **`/blog/polaris-slingshot-top-speed/`** — "polaris slingshot top speed" (1,300 US). Highest-volume keyword in the entire informational set, near-zero commercial competition (no rental business ranks for it today), pure content play.
4. **`/slingshot-rental/` (pillar)** — "polaris slingshot rental" (1,900 US, KD 11) is materially easier than the broader "slingshot rental" (KD 28) while sharing the same page; the incumbent DFW leader (dallasslingshotrental.com) has a thin site (homepage + one blog post), so a genuinely built pillar with schema/FAQ can outcompete it structurally, not just on content depth.
5. **`/slingshot-rental/date-night/`** — "slingshot experience" (KD 1, peaks April — matches the business's actual operating window exactly) combined with the wide-open "date night/date ideas Dallas" cluster (2,400–2,900 TX volume, zero slingshot presence in any SERP or AI Overview pulled). Names the offering honestly ("experience," not "rental fleet") while tapping real, large adjacent demand.

**Deprioritized/flagged:** `/slingshot-rental/near-arlington/` (SERP overlap of 6–7 with Dallas — same operators, borderline "same post") and `/slingshot-rental/waxahachie-ellis-county/` (zero independent SERP opportunity, zero measurable search volume) should be built thin/fast, not treated as content-investment priorities, despite being in the site's URL plan.
