## 4. Positioning changes to make everywhere

1. **Lead with the Welcome Center.** "We meet you at the Ennis Welcome Center, 201 NW Main — the same place the trail maps are handed out." It answers "where," borrows the city's trust, and captures 3,600 April searches for "ennis welcome center."
2. **Lead with the law.** "No motorcycle license. Your regular Texas driver's license is all you need (Tex. Transp. Code §521.085)." Competitors still get this wrong in Google's AI Overview.
3. **Name the friction we remove.** No deposit, insurance included, no pickup logistics, 5-minute orientation + supervised test loop. These are exactly what 5-star reviews praise across the category.
4. **Fix the spec.** Polaris ProStar 2.0L four-cylinder, 180 hp (S/SL) or 204 hp (SLR/R). "1.5L twin, 203 hp" is wrong and will get quoted.
5. **Two-seat honesty.** Passenger doesn't need approval; drivers 21+ (**DECIDE** exact minimum age and passenger minimum age/weight); 275 lb/seat is the category norm.
